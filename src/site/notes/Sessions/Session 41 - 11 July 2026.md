**Date In-Game:** Continues directly from Session 40 - morning through to evening of the same day

## Key Events

- Concluded the battle against [[NPCs/Lord Pompaduke the Third\|Lord Pompaduke the Third]] and the zombie horde in [[Locations/Thalas'Miriel\|Thalas'Miriel]] town centre, begun at the end of Session 40
- [[NPCs/Ser Alric Vayne\|Ser Alric Vayne]] charged into the fray early on - running one zombie through, decapitating a second with a natural 20 and max damage, then shield-bashing and finishing off a third, rolling two natural 20s across the turn
- [[NPCs/Calen Thorne\|Calen Thorne]], swarmed and near death, unleashed his previously unused Ice Blade ability - encasing himself and the nearby combatants (including [[NPCs/Ser Alric Vayne\|Ser Alric Vayne]] and [[Party Members/Elvva Beltavar\|Elvva Beltavar]]) in ice for 34 points of cold damage, which cleared the zombies then on the board
- A fresh pair of zombies closed in on the downed [[Party Members/Elvva Beltavar\|Elvva Beltavar]]; [[NPCs/Ser Alric Vayne\|Ser Alric Vayne]] smashed free of Calen's ice and decapitated both with two clean slashes
- [[Party Members/Ser Tessell Highfrost\|Ser Tessell Highfrost]] stabilised the party with Channel Divinity: Preserve Life and Mass Healing Word, distributing healing between [[NPCs/Calen Thorne\|Calen Thorne]], [[Party Members/Elvva Beltavar\|Elvva Beltavar]] and - out of curiosity - a bloodied zombie (confirmed to have no effect, per the current edition's rules)
- [[Party Members/Lady Odonia of Galantri\|Lady Odonia of Galantri]]'s Infinite Radiant Bow felled what proved to be [[NPCs/Lord Pompaduke the Third\|Lord Pompaduke the Third]]'s decoy; his true, invisible form was elsewhere on the battlefield the whole time. He revealed himself, bowed theatrically, and portalled away with a taunting "See you at our next chapter!"
- The party retreated to the town's barricaded tavern, now serving as a makeshift field hospital for roughly 50 townsfolk, run by [[NPCs/Alarin Moonwhisper\|Alarin Moonwhisper]] and the other squires
- [[Party Members/Lady Odonia of Galantri\|Lady Odonia of Galantri]], [[Party Members/Elvva Beltavar\|Elvva Beltavar]], [[Party Members/Elik'r Stormswallow\|Elik'r Stormswallow]] and [[Party Members/Ser Tessell Highfrost\|Ser Tessell Highfrost]] took a long rest; [[Party Members/Tooo-Ah\|Tooo-Ah]] stayed awake, tending to frightened children and townsfolk (attempting, unsuccessfully, to argue that flute practice alone should count towards improving her Charisma)
- [[NPCs/Calen Thorne\|Calen Thorne]] confirmed to [[Party Members/Tooo-Ah\|Tooo-Ah]] that he has decided to formally claim the Ducal throne of Neverwinter, though he was evasive about what prompted the decision
- [[Party Members/Tooo-Ah\|Tooo-Ah]] asked [[NPCs/Ser Alric Vayne\|Ser Alric Vayne]] whether the Order of the Silver Lantern might back [[NPCs/Calen Thorne\|Calen Thorne]]'s claim; no firm answer given, though he later declined to spare any warriors to escort the party to the cemetery, as the town's defence takes priority
- [[Party Members/Elik'r Stormswallow\|Elik'r Stormswallow]] spoke to the Steve stone in the tavern; the voice that replied was recognisably [[NPCs/Co'narr 'Banewatcher' Hyborian\|Co'narr 'Banewatcher' Hyborian]], who warned against trusting [[NPCs/Lord Pompaduke the Third\|Lord Pompaduke the Third]] and revealed he was cast out of Co'narr's order many decades ago (Elik'r's own in-character understanding of what/who Steve is remains unresolved)
- The party rode out to a cemetery to the south-west of town - the source of the zombie horde, previously glimpsed by [[Party Members/Tooo-Ah\|Tooo-Ah]] as a green, Bane-tinged haze while flying to the earlier battle
- At an old mausoleum within the cemetery, the party found four figures in a clandestine meeting: the [[NPCs/Ember Vein Envoy - Unnamed\|Ember Vein Envoy - unnamed]], [[NPCs/Lord Pompaduke the Third\|Lord Pompaduke the Third]], an unnamed ornately-dressed man wielding a cane that conceals a sword, and the [[NPCs/Unknown Axe Fighter\|Unknown Axe Fighter]], wielding a massive hammer or axe
- The [[NPCs/Ember Vein Envoy - Unnamed\|Ember Vein Envoy - unnamed]] revealed that "the other dragon you awakened" is located on **Dragonclaw Isle**, before declaring their side's "time is over here" and departing for Neverwinter
- [[NPCs/Lord Pompaduke the Third\|Lord Pompaduke the Third]] bowed deeply and portalled away a second time this session
- The unnamed cane-sword man - previously present in the town square when [[Party Members/Elik'r Stormswallow\|Elik'r Stormswallow]] was forced to kill [[Party Members/Elvva Beltavar\|Elvva Beltavar]] and when [[Guy\|Guy]] died - cast a necromancy spell, sending four bolts of green Bane light off the map before vanishing
- The [[NPCs/Unknown Axe Fighter\|Unknown Axe Fighter]] remained, challenging the party with "let's see what the famed warriors can do" before the session ended on the initiative roll
