## Key Events

### Elvva's Perspective - Trapped in the Cathedral

From [[Party Members/Elvva Beltavar\|Elvva Beltavar]]'s point of view, the previous two sessions played out very differently. Seated apart from the rest of the party in the [[Locations/Cathedral of Gentle Redemption\|Cathedral of Gentle Redemption]], she found herself pinned to the pew by an elderly nun seated beside her - a woman with a vice-like grip and what might charitably be described as a strength rating well in excess of twenty. No saving throw, no roll, no escape: Elvva was obliged to sit through the entire sermon.

She took twenty points of psychic damage from the experience.

A few hours in, she noticed a number of guards running through the cathedral and into a side room. Shortly afterwards, as bells began ringing throughout the building, she felt a strange sensation - and erupted into green flames. When the fire cleared, she found herself standing in an underground ceremony room with the rest of the party to her left. In front of them stood a hooded woman. Beyond the woman, a towering stone statue of a purple-scaled dragonborn warrior had just begun to stir.

### Recap - Sessions 37 and 38 (Party Perspective)

With Elvva now present, the party filled in the gaps. [[Party Members/Tooo-Ah\|Tooo-Ah]]'s attempt to steal coins from the tithing bag during the cathedral service in Session 37 had led to her arrest, the rest of the party following to break her out of the [[Organisations/Order of the Silver Lantern\|Order of the Silver Lantern]] garrison, [[NPCs/Brother Calen\|Brother Calen]] offering to guide them in exchange for help finding [[NPCs/Clara\|Clara]], and a full infiltration - during which [[Party Members/Ser Tessell Highfrost\|Ser Tessell Highfrost]] became briefly entangled in a bush, a garrison guard was killed with a precisely placed thrown dagger by [[NPCs/Calen Thorne\|Brother Calen]], and Tessell and Calen disguised themselves in stolen guard armour. The party descended via a blood-activated symbol into the [[Locations/Ember Vein Underground Complex\|Ember Vein Underground Complex]], passed through a draining corridor and a draconic archway, explored four side rooms containing cult letters, tomes, a torture chamber, and a dead man's journal, and touched the ember fissures - receiving separate psychic visions from the seraph (Elik'r receiving _"Release,"_ Tessell receiving _"Kill them all"_).

They then entered the ritual chamber, killed eight robed cultists (Tessell's Spirit Guardians accounting for the majority), fought and killed [[NPCs/Darius Kain\|Darius Kain]] - the corrupted High Commander of the [[Organisations/Order of the Silver Lantern\|Order of the Silver Lantern]] - and stopped the sacrifice. The [[NPCs/Ember Vein Envoy - Unnamed\|Ember Vein Envoy - Unnamed]] revealed a hidden back wall, restored the party to full health and spell slots, teleported [[Party Members/Elvva Beltavar\|Elvva Beltavar]] directly into the chamber from the cathedral, and spoke a single word: _"Arise."_

The stone statue cracked, pulsed orange, and shed its shell. The [[NPCs/Purple Dragonborn\|Purple Dragonborn]] lived.

### The Purple Dragonborn - "You Carry My Line"

The [[NPCs/Purple Dragonborn\|Purple Dragonborn]] stands twelve feet tall, purple-scaled, armoured in heavy plate, his longsword still gripped in gauntleted hands with trails of molten light dripping from the blade's cracks. His first words as he surveys the five figures before him are not a roar but something closer to disbelief: _"No. It was sealed. I ended it. I gave everything."_

As his gaze travels along the line of the party - left to right, Elvva, Tooo-Ah, Tessell, Odonia - it stops one place short of the end. He turns back, and fixes his eyes on [[Party Members/Elik'r Stormswallow\|Elik'r Stormswallow]]. Recognition moves across his face, followed almost immediately by anger.

_"You carry my line. And yet you undo my sacrifice."_

Next to the statue, propped against the wall, hangs a banner: a purple dragon bound by chains, mounted on a long shaft of gold and red wood. [[Party Members/Elik'r Stormswallow\|Elik'r Stormswallow]] recognises it immediately as his family's long-lost standard.

[[Party Members/Elik'r Stormswallow\|Elik'r Stormswallow]] steps forward. _"I know not of who you are or what you have sacrificed."_ The dragonborn's grip on his sword tightens. His answer comes as a declaration to the room:

_"I bound the wyrm beneath the world. Burned my name into stone so it would never rise again. And now you awake it. If the seal is broken, then I will do what I was made to do. I will end this threat. Even if that threat is you."_

He raises his blade. Flames erupt along the steel.

_Roll for initiative._

### Combat - Phase One

**Initiative order:** [[Party Members/Tooo-Ah\|Tooo-Ah]] (22), [[Party Members/Elik'r Stormswallow\|Elik'r Stormswallow]] (19), [[Party Members/Lady Odonia of Galantri\|Lady Odonia of Galantri]] (14), [[Party Members/Ser Tessell Highfrost\|Ser Tessell Highfrost]] (10), [[Party Members/Elvva Beltavar\|Elvva Beltavar]] (9).

The [[NPCs/Purple Dragonborn\|Purple Dragonborn]] moves first, attacking [[Party Members/Elik'r Stormswallow\|Elik'r Stormswallow]] with two longsword strikes. The first is parried cleanly off Elik'r's shield; the second falls too wide and smashes down into his shoulder - six points of slashing damage, plus four points of fire damage, halved to two by Elik'r's fire resistance. As the blow lands: _"You're not ready to bear this legacy."_

**[[Party Members/Tooo-Ah\|Tooo-Ah]]** flies in and strikes the dragonborn twice with her fists, landing both blows and spending a ki point on Stunning Strike after the first. The strike connects. The dragonborn does not look stunned. He doesn't look particularly bothered. He turns his head and regards her with something approaching surprise: _"You can harm me?"_ [[Party Members/Tooo-Ah\|Tooo-Ah]] is undeterred. She deals a further kick and confirms: _"I can and I will."_ She then indicates [[Party Members/Elik'r Stormswallow\|Elik'r Stormswallow]]: _"He may be a thick lunk, but I owe him a debt and I will protect him."_ She subsequently disengages and puts some distance between herself and the dragonborn. (Her bludgeoning damage, it will become clear, is halved against this target - a significant impediment to her preferred fighting approach.)

**[[Party Members/Elik'r Stormswallow\|Elik'r Stormswallow]]** delivers a short statement - _"I know not of who you are or what you have sacrificed"_ - immediately forgets his plan, and swings his sword. He misses. Catching himself, he casts Vow of Enmity (Channel Divinity), granting advantage on all attacks against his target for the duration, then follows up with a proper strike: 21 to hit, landing for eight points of slashing damage plus seven points of necrotic damage from Wrathful Smite. He demands the dragonborn succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or suffer the Frightened condition. The dragonborn does not. The response to Elik'r's statement comes nonetheless: _"You do not understand what you've broken."_

**[[Party Members/Lady Odonia of Galantri\|Lady Odonia of Galantri]]** moves up and attacks with the Twin Blades of the Serpent, using the Whirling Fang Strike for eight points of slashing damage. She follows up with Ensnaring Strike - vines rise from the stone to grapple the dragonborn, are met with his flames, and burn away. He beat the strength saving throw with ease.

**[[Party Members/Elvva Beltavar\|Elvva Beltavar]]** casts Hex (bonus action) and hurls a Chromatic Orb at level two - poison damage, eighteen to hit, connecting for twenty total points of damage including Hex. The poisonous orb melts through his armour plating with notable efficiency. The dragonborn observes the result: _"Oh, good. Show me your strength."_

**[[Party Members/Ser Tessell Highfrost\|Ser Tessell Highfrost]]** casts Spirit Guardians and Spiritual Weapon, directing the latter into a strike for ten points of force damage (21 to hit). He manoeuvres so the dragonborn falls within his fifteen-foot Spirit Guardian emanation - the dragonborn passes the DC 14 Wisdom saving throw - then speaks: _"Should we be attacking you, or trying to help you seal this thing away?"_ The roll is 21 Persuasion. The dragonborn considers. His answer comes later.

**[[NPCs/Calen Thorne\|Brother Calen]]** runs back into the chamber and throws a dagger, connecting cleanly. At the same moment, a different sound reaches the room from the far side - a lute, playing something low and ominous. The sound becomes visible: from the shadows, Lord Pompaduke the Third appears, lute in hand, performing what can only be described as villain music for the occasion. "Banjo Man," [[Party Members/Tooo-Ah\|Tooo-Ah]] shouts across the chamber. Noted.

The [[NPCs/Purple Dragonborn\|Purple Dragonborn]]'s turn: he activates **Blazing Step**, moving at half speed and dealing four points of fire damage (two for [[Party Members/Elik'r Stormswallow\|Elik'r Stormswallow]], halved by resistance) to every creature he passes within five feet - [[Party Members/Ser Tessell Highfrost\|Ser Tessell Highfrost]], [[Party Members/Elik'r Stormswallow\|Elik'r Stormswallow]], [[NPCs/Calen Thorne\|Brother Calen]], and [[Party Members/Tooo-Ah\|Tooo-Ah]] - without provoking opportunity attacks. He then turns on [[Party Members/Elvva Beltavar\|Elvva Beltavar]] and strikes twice with his flaming longsword - the second blow a critical hit. She takes twenty-eight points of damage (twenty-two slashing, six fire). As he breathes and looks across the charred remains scattered at his feet: _"I remember this fire. The same fire that I used to fell it."_ He looks back at [[Party Members/Ser Tessell Highfrost\|Ser Tessell Highfrost]], answering the earlier question at last: _"Let me remind you what it takes to end the dragon."_

Tessell takes this to mean they must fight. Elik'r agrees. _"Yeah. Did you not remember - this is a test."_

**[[Party Members/Tooo-Ah\|Tooo-Ah]]** reaches into her wings and draws out three magical feather darts - her special character ability, capable of being targeted at a single creature or split across three. She targets all three at the [[NPCs/Purple Dragonborn\|Purple Dragonborn]]. First dart: 19 to hit - lands. Second: 16 - lands. Third: natural twenty.

She rolled 3d6 for the critical dart's damage. The dragonborn, who had regarded this aarakocra's attacks as a curiosity, staggers. The room falls briefly quiet.

The [[NPCs/Purple Dragonborn\|Purple Dragonborn]] has taken thirty-two points of damage from a creature he told _"You can harm me?"_

He collapses - and then does not stay down.

### Phase Two - Stage 2

As the dragonborn falls, his body cracks open along every seam. Green Bane-fire floods out through the fractures. [[NPCs/Lord Pompaduke the Third\|Lord Pompaduke the Third]] leans into his lute. The party feel the music pulling at them - almost physically, as though the sound itself is tugging them towards him. The song waves wash over the dragonborn's crumpled form.

_"The fire, it rises again,"_ the dragonborn says, his voice changing. _"This heat, this pain. I remember dying in this."_

Green flame erupts around him. [[NPCs/Lord Pompaduke the Third\|Lord Pompaduke the Third]] shouts across the chamber to [[Party Members/Tooo-Ah\|Tooo-Ah]]: _"Have fun!"_ - wraps his cape about himself, glows green, and vanishes.

The dragonborn erupts in a psychic roar. Every party member except [[Party Members/Lady Odonia of Galantri\|Lady Odonia of Galantri]] (safely out of range) must make a Dexterity saving throw - [[Party Members/Tooo-Ah\|Tooo-Ah]] (natural twenty, twenty-five total), [[Party Members/Elik'r Stormswallow\|Elik'r Stormswallow]] (twenty-one), [[Party Members/Elvva Beltavar\|Elvva Beltavar]] (sixteen), [[NPCs/Calen Thorne\|Brother Calen]] (natural twenty), and [[Party Members/Ser Tessell Highfrost\|Ser Tessell Highfrost]] (natural but low - five total, unmodified). Tessell takes the full twenty-seven points of psychic damage. Everyone else takes thirteen. [[Party Members/Elvva Beltavar\|Elvva Beltavar]] and [[NPCs/Calen Thorne\|Brother Calen]] are both reduced to one hit point.

---

**[[Party Members/Elik'r Stormswallow\|Elik'r Stormswallow]]** strikes twice with his longsword, Hunter's Mark active for additional force damage alongside two castings of Thunderous Smite. The thunder crashes through the room - 300-foot radius of audible impact - and the dragonborn recoils. He appears notably more susceptible to thunder damage than to fire or bludgeoning. Total damage: forty-two points.

The dragonborn is bloodied.

He looks at [[Party Members/Elik'r Stormswallow\|Elik'r Stormswallow]]. _"You. You're the last proof I existed. If you fail, my sacrifice dies with you. It's down to you. Show me my bloodline was not a mistake. Finish me now before it takes over."_ The Bane courses through him visibly. _"Just remember - when I fall, the dragon rises. If you do manage to defeat me, then perhaps-"_ He stops speaking. Initiative continues.

**[[Party Members/Lady Odonia of Galantri\|Lady Odonia of Galantri]]** first asks the dragonborn for his name - _"I pray thee, what is thy name?"_ - before firing two longbow shots. Both hit for a combined fifteen points of piercing and radiant damage. She attempts Ensnaring Strike on the back of a hit; the dragonborn beats the strength saving throw. He does not answer her question. He does not have the chance to.

**[[Party Members/Elvva Beltavar\|Elvva Beltavar]]**, at one hit point, Misty Steps twenty feet away from the dragonborn's immediate reach (bonus action), then hurls a Lightning Chromatic Orb at level three - advantage from Simmering Magic active, white dice roll of five. The orb of crackling lightning smashes into the dragonborn. Twenty-three points of lightning damage, plus three from Hex, multiplied because of his notable weakness to lightning: forty-nine points total. He has received twenty-six points from Chromatic Orb damage alone and it is doubled.

The electricity courses through his armour and chars him.

He collapses. This time, he does not rise.

### The Dragonborn's Last Words

The [[NPCs/Purple Dragonborn\|Purple Dragonborn]] falls close to [[Party Members/Elik'r Stormswallow\|Elik'r Stormswallow]]. He looks up - something in the expression reminiscent of a man finally released from a very long sentence - and speaks his final words.

_"The seal is weakening. You must finish it, before it's too late. Take this blade."_

He holds out the longsword. As [[Party Members/Elik'r Stormswallow\|Elik'r Stormswallow]] takes hold of it, the weapon shifts - contracting down from a blade sized for a twelve-foot warrior to a longsword Elik'r can wield. The blade is dark iron, threaded through with ember-red veins.

_"Do what I could not do, for the sake of our family in the world."_

A last gesture - towards the banner, the purple dragon bound in chains, mounted on its gold and red shaft.

_"One of our family left that behind. That is a symbol of what our job is - what our family's right is to be."_

His body does not bleed out. It turns back to stone. And then it crumbles to dust.

### The Wyrm Rises - Lord Pompaduke Reveals His Hand

The warning proves immediately accurate. The rumbling that has been building throughout the fight intensifies. The floor beneath [[Party Members/Elik'r Stormswallow\|Elik'r Stormswallow]] and [[NPCs/Calen Thorne\|Brother Calen]] begins to give way - [[NPCs/Calen Thorne\|Brother Calen]] keeps his feet; Elik'r fails his Dexterity saving throw and falls prone as the stone splits.

From beneath the ground, something climbs out. A wyrm - wingless, deep red, its body plated in an armoured, orange-golden hue - rises from the fissure and roars.

And then, as though he had been invisible all along (which, it emerges, is precisely what happened), [[NPCs/Lord Pompaduke the Third\|Lord Pompaduke the Third]] materialises close to the dragon. He performs his customary over-the-top bow to the party. He is every inch the showman.

_"Thank you, everyone, for completing the task I needed to do. For me."_

He plays a brief passage on his lute - the technique closely resembling what [[Party Members/Ser Tessell Highfrost\|Ser Tessell Highfrost]] has seen before when Pompaduke opened a portal. A gateway opens in the air beside him, rimmed with the same green Bane-fire that has coloured every surface of this complex. He guides the wyrm through it and steps in after. The portal closes.

The green flames in the ritual chamber's corners gutter and die. The room goes still.

### Aftermath - Looting the Chamber

Out of initiative, the party take stock. [[Party Members/Ser Tessell Highfrost\|Ser Tessell Highfrost]] heals what he can. [[Party Members/Elik'r Stormswallow\|Elik'r Stormswallow]] claims the family banner immediately. The remaining fires having gone out, there is time to examine the chamber properly.

[[Party Members/Lady Odonia of Galantri\|Lady Odonia of Galantri]] and [[Party Members/Tooo-Ah\|Tooo-Ah]] come away with nothing - an investigation roll of three and a natural one respectively, the latter earning [[Party Members/Tooo-Ah\|Tooo-Ah]] the ignominy of almost rolling [[NPCs/Darius Kain\|Darius Kain]]'s body into the hole in the floor before the DM's mercy saves her. [[Party Members/Elvva Beltavar\|Elvva Beltavar]] rolls sixteen, [[Party Members/Elik'r Stormswallow\|Elik'r Stormswallow]] thirteen, and [[Party Members/Ser Tessell Highfrost\|Ser Tessell Highfrost]] twenty.

**Tessell's investigation** focuses on [[NPCs/Darius Kain\|Darius Kain]]'s body. The mace [[NPCs/Darius Kain\|Darius Kain]] was wielding proves, on close arcane inspection, to be a standard steel weapon - no innate magical properties, though it could sell. More significantly, Tessell finds a sealed scroll on Darius's person - what appears to be written orders of some kind, still unread - and on his hands, two rings worn on the same finger: one in darker metal, one lighter, each set with a deep red stone (ruby or garnet, unclear at this distance) within a swirling carved mount. Both are magical. Tessell identifies them as **Blood-Pact Rings**.

The rings function as a matched pair: once per day, if one wearer drops to zero hit points, they instead drop to one. The other wearer, however, takes double the damage that would have dropped their partner. The question of why [[NPCs/Darius Kain\|Darius Kain]] was wearing both simultaneously is left, for now, unanswered.

**Elik'r's investigation** of the makeshift office in the corner of the main chamber - clearly used as a command space for whoever was coordinating the ritual - turns up, in a chest, a small gold key. In the centre of the key's bow sits a tiny green eye. Elik'r identifies it. The key is a **Key of the Wrong Door**: once per long rest, the holder may insert it into any locked door and turn it - then roll 1d6. A 1 causes the door to explode. A 2 or 3 leaves it stubbornly locked with no negative outcome. A 4 or 5 opens it normally. A 6 opens the door to a completely different location chosen by the DM. A note from Tessell: this key does not supersede a door sealed by his own magical lock picks. Elik'r disputes this vigorously, yet incorrectly.

**Elvva's investigation** of the cultists in the chamber turns up, on one of them, a single tarnished silver coin. It is the **Coin of Fate**: once per long rest, when the holder hits a creature with an attack, they may flip the coin. Heads - the target takes double the damage. Tails - the holder takes the damage instead.

The silver and copper from the bodies is divided: Tessell (thirteen silver, nine copper), Elik'r (fourteen silver, eight copper), Elvva (eight silver, five copper).

### Interrogating Calen Thorne

Before the group fast-travel to [[NPCs/Calen Thorne\|Brother Calen]]'s hovel for a long rest, [[Party Members/Elvva Beltavar\|Elvva Beltavar]] introduces herself to him. [[Party Members/Lady Odonia of Galantri\|Lady Odonia of Galantri]] asks him directly: what has been his involvement with the [[NPCs/The Bane\|Bane]], and where did his fighting ability actually come from? He seems like considerably more than a monk. She rolls persuasion (fourteen with penalty, [[Party Members/Ser Tessell Highfrost\|Ser Tessell Highfrost]] assisting with eight). It is sufficient.

[[NPCs/Calen Thorne\|Brother Calen]] confirms what the party had already pieced together. He was sent to [[Locations/Thalas'Miriel\|Thalas'Miriel]] by his father to remain out of sight and out of mind. He is the youngest bastard son of the [[NPCs/Duke Alaric Thorne\|Duke of Neverwinter]]. His father is an old man, and - as far as Calen last knew - dying. The eldest legitimate son, **Edron**, is effectively in charge now: a man who wants to preserve what his father built but is, by reputation, cold-hearted and deeply ambitious. The middle son, **Marek**, is the firebrand - a knight who left court years ago to become a mercenary, charismatic and daring, who resents political games but craves power. Marek has apparently returned, bringing a mercenary company with him. Its origin is unknown.

[[Party Members/Tooo-Ah\|Tooo-Ah]] asks about the Ducal succession. If the Duke dies without naming an heir, it does not automatically pass to the eldest - it falls to a Regent and council to decide. The current Regent is a Professor from abroad, apparently wise and reportedly very gaudy, who rose through the ranks after the Duke's condition worsened. [[NPCs/Calen Thorne\|Brother Calen]] does not know his name; the appointment came after Calen had already been sent away.

As for his own fighting ability - it comes from his early years as a squire and page at court in [[Locations/Neverwinter\|Neverwinter]]. He also mentions, in passing, a knight he befriended - presumably [[NPCs/Ser Alric Vayne\|Ser Alric Vayne]] the same knight the party befriended - who has since taught him further skills in arms, which explains his affinity with the uncorrupted members of the [[Organisations/Order of the Silver Lantern\|Order of the Silver Lantern]].

**[[Party Members/Elik'r Stormswallow\|Elik'r Stormswallow]]** asks the question that has been hovering: would [[NPCs/Calen Thorne\|Brother Calen]] be willing to take the Ducal title, should he be given the chance? And whose side would he take in the conflict?

[[NPCs/Calen Thorne\|Brother Calen]] turns the question back: _"Why are you asking me this? Whose side are you on?"_ [[Party Members/Elik'r Stormswallow\|Elik'r Stormswallow]] answers: the side of life. [[NPCs/Calen Thorne\|Brother Calen]] points out, with some accuracy, that they have killed rather a lot of people, maimed someone in a tournament, and generally accumulated what he politely terms "a lot of accidents." He is not wrong. The party acknowledge this and note that they are now identified, chivalric, and hold renown. [[Party Members/Lady Odonia of Galantri\|Lady Odonia of Galantri]] has a village.

The honest answer to why Calen specifically is that he is, as [[Party Members/Elik'r Stormswallow\|Elik'r Stormswallow]] puts it, "the most predictable of the three brothers" - though this is not said to his face.

[[Party Members/Tooo-Ah\|Tooo-Ah]] offers a final thought: if both brothers were to take sides against the throne, there might be an opening for a new Duke.

[[NPCs/Calen Thorne\|Brother Calen]] considers this. _"I'll have an answer for you by morning."_

## Memorable Moments

- During the Session 37 recap, [[Party Members/Tooo-Ah\|Tooo-Ah]] is revealed to have introduced the party to [[NPCs/Ser Alric Vayne\|Ser Alric Vayne]] in prison as _"Tooo-Ah of the Swallow Brigade"_ - having completely forgotten the name "Riders of the Storm" in the moment. The improvised name reduced the DM to fits of laughter and was described as the best moment of the campaign to date.
- [[Party Members/Elik'r Stormswallow\|Elik'r Stormswallow]] delivers a speech to the [[NPCs/Purple Dragonborn\|Purple Dragonborn]] - "I know not of who you are or what you have sacrificed" - and then immediately swings and misses because he forgot to cast Vow of Enmity first.
- [[Party Members/Ser Tessell Highfrost\|Ser Tessell Highfrost]] casts Mass Healing Word and announces: _"So you can have sixteen points of damage."_ The DM lets a pause stretch and then responds: _"Finally, I hit everybody!"_
- [[Party Members/Tooo-Ah\|Tooo-Ah]], having declared with considerable flair that she owes [[Party Members/Elik'r Stormswallow\|Elik'r Stormswallow]] a debt and will protect him, immediately disengages and retreats several squares from the fight. [[Party Members/Elik'r Stormswallow\|Elik'r Stormswallow]] notes that this is not obviously what protection looks like.
- The [[NPCs/Purple Dragonborn\|Purple Dragonborn]]'s dying revelation - _"You carry my line"_ - confirms that [[Party Members/Elik'r Stormswallow\|Elik'r Stormswallow]]'s bloodline descends from an ancient warrior who bound a wyrm beneath the world, sacrificed himself to keep it sealed, and spent centuries entombed in stone calling Elik'r's name. The family banner was waiting the whole time.
- [[NPCs/Lord Pompaduke the Third\|Lord Pompaduke the Third]]'s curtain-call bow and _"Thank you, everyone, for completing the task I needed to do. For me"_ - delivered as he steps through a Bane portal with the party's wyrm - is received with the exact mix of awe and betrayal it deserves.
