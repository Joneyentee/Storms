**Location:** [[Locations/Thalas'Miriel Cemetery\|Thalas'Miriel Cemetery]] → [[Locations/Tomb of Ser Elenai\|Tomb of Ser Elenai]] → [[Locations/Thalas'Miriel\|Thalas'Miriel]] → road towards [[Locations/Neverwinter\|Neverwinter]] **Date In-Game:** Continues directly from Session 42 (same evening) through the following night and morning

## Key Events

- **Pompaduke leaves.** [[NPCs/Lord Pompaduke the Third\|Lord Pompaduke the Third]] ("Banjo Man") stepped out at the mausoleum and gave [[Party Members/Elik'r Stormswallow\|Elik'r Stormswallow]] a message for [[NPCs/Co'narr 'Banewatcher' Hyborian\|Co'narr 'Banewatcher' Hyborian]]: _"You can tell that stubborn oaf I'm coming for him next."_ He then portalled away in a cloud of green mist, using [[NPCs/The Bane\|The Bane]] as he went so that every remaining skeleton collapsed where it stood
- **The S42 Wisdom save resolved.** The effect rolled at the end of Session 42 was a compulsion on [[Party Members/Elik'r Stormswallow\|Elik'r Stormswallow]] and [[Party Members/Lady Odonia of Galantri\|Lady Odonia of Galantri]] to attack [[Party Members/Ser Tessell Highfrost\|Ser Tessell Highfrost]]: Elik'r punched him with his sword hilt (natural 20 to hit, unarmed damage); Odonia went to slap him and Tessell caught her hand
- **A voice for Tessell.** A Religion check (17) let Tessell alone hear a goddess's voice telling him to go straight ahead
- **The door.** The mausoleum door bore the heraldry of the [[Organisations/Order of the Silver Lantern\|Order of the Silver Lantern]] - the lantern from their tabards, shields and banners. Tessell felt the urge to pray, prayed and knocked; the lantern glowed and the stone doors swung open (into his face). There was no tomb inside, only a staircase leading down - the real mausoleum lies below. Calen came too, mainly because he did not want to be left alone. The doors ground shut behind the party
- **The tomb.** A vast circular chamber, undisturbed for centuries and lit by light glowing through cracks in the ceiling. At its centre stands a large statue of a regal female knight in ceremonial armour with a spiked crown, holding an enormous glaive whose head is a lantern frame around a glowing stone. Five smaller statues stand around the outer wall, each with a smoother stone plate in front of it. [[NPCs/Calen Thorne\|Calen Thorne]] identified the name carved there - Ser Elenai - as the founder of the Order. See [[Locations/Tomb of Ser Elenai\|Tomb of Ser Elenai]]
- **The prophecy.** When Tessell prayed for guidance, the central statue turned to face him and a voice in everyone's heads told him to come closer, then recited a prophecy written for Tessell (full text in [[Locations/Tomb of Ser Elenai\|Tomb of Ser Elenai]]). Ant's summary: centuries ago it was foretold that someone called Tessell - with poetic licence over the name "Highfrost" - would face this trial and must complete five tasks with the help of others. Ant said he wrote this quest line for Paul a long time ago
- **The five trials.** Each outer statue was a test, and the party took one each:
  - **Vigilance - the one who sees** (statue 1, blindfolded figure beside a pedestal of carved stars): [[Party Members/Tooo-Ah\|Tooo-Ah]] flew up, counted 13 stars on the ceiling against 12 on the pedestal, and pressed the extra star like a button. The statue's blindfold fell and silver light shot from its eyes into the central lantern
  - **Mercy - the one who gives** (statue 2, kneeling figure with an offering bowl): [[Party Members/Ser Tessell Highfrost\|Ser Tessell Highfrost]] stood on the plate, the statue lifted its bowl, and he filled it until it overflowed - 100 silver in all
  - **Courage - the one who stands** (statue 3, the knight with a lantern on her shield): [[NPCs/Calen Thorne\|Calen Thorne]] stepped up first and was forced to one knee by a hurricane-strength force; [[Party Members/Elik'r Stormswallow\|Elik'r Stormswallow]] pulled him off (Strength save) and took his place, crushed as if in a bear hug, then forced himself back to his feet (Constitution save 20). The statue slammed its shield down
  - **Truth - the one who reveals** (statue 4, figure holding a tarnished mirror): [[Party Members/Elvva Beltavar\|Elvva Beltavar]] turned the mirror to throw the light onto the central lantern; the mirror shattered
  - **Sacrifice - the one who lets go** (statue 5, figure clutching a stone lantern with its other hand empty): [[Party Members/Lady Odonia of Galantri\|Lady Odonia of Galantri]] first placed a silver coin, then her eye patch, in the open hand - both times it erupted in fire (the second for 28 fire damage, reduced with Absorb Elements), and the eye patch was destroyed. She succeeded when she said aloud what she was holding on to: _"I'm the Princess's assassin and I killed the king."_ - in front of the whole party and Calen
- **The lantern.** All five statues glowed in different colours. The central lantern spoke with pulses of light: _"You have seen, you have given, you have stood, you have revealed, you have let go"_ - and asked what a lantern is for. Tessell answered (a lantern guides others through the dark), the lantern erupted in silver-white light, the five statues knelt towards the centre and the floor shook
- **Ser Elenai.** A drawer-like sarcophagus opened in the base of the central statue, revealing the body of Ser Elenai in the ceremonial armour of the Silver Lantern. The core in her glaive began to shine, the glaive rose, and her voice addressed Tessell directly:

> _"Tessell Highfrost, half-elf, a child of two worlds. Champion of the questionable decisions. I have watched you, Tessell. I have seen you charge towards a castle with the courage of a thousand knights only to find the earth rather unexpectedly beneath you. I have heard whispers of your dealings with a certain Countess, though I confess I remain uncertain whether you were pursuing her affection or narrowly escaping her hospitality._
>
> _And yet I have also seen you stand where others would fall. I have seen you show a kindness where none was owed and a courage where none was expected. Do you know what makes a lantern worthy of its flame, Tessell? It is not the beauty nor the strength of its flame, nor even the brightness of its light; it is the darkness it is willing to enter so that another might find their way._
>
> _You are no perfect knight, Tessell Highfrost, nor would I wish you to be so. You stumble, you stray, you make rather spectacular mistakes, but you rise, and when another has fallen, you do not leave them there - unlike some in your party, I may wonder. That, Tessell, is the heart of the Silver Lanterns. I carried this light through the long nights. I held it when hope was little more than a fading ember. Now I entrust it to you - not because you are the greatest warrior, not because you are the wisest cleric out there, but because somewhere beneath that wonderfully troublesome exterior there burns a light worth following. Rise, Tessell Highfrost. The First Lantern's watch has just ended. Yours has just begun._
>
> _And Tessell, should you encounter that Countess again, try not to fall at her feet. It rather undermines this occasion, I should think."_

- The glaive flew into Tessell's hand like Thor's hammer - [[Items/The First Lantern's Glaive\|The First Lantern's Glaive]] - and the central statue crumbled
- **Back to Thalas'Miriel.** The party returned to town that night; the zombies near the gates had crumbled to dust. The Order's veteran knights recognised the glaive. [[NPCs/Ser Alric Vayne\|Ser Alric Vayne]] knew exactly what it was: by the legends passed down through the Order's leadership, it would never be handed to someone unwillingly, and whoever is granted it takes on being the **"First Light"**. Ant was clear that this does not make Tessell leader of the Order unless he chooses to challenge for it
- **Calen's claim.** On the walk to the tavern, [[NPCs/Calen Thorne\|Calen Thorne]] set out his family's position: his father [[NPCs/Duke Alaric Thorne\|Duke Alaric Thorne]] supports the Crown by default as a noble, but is an old man on his last legs; [[NPCs/Edran Thorne\|Edran Thorne]] effectively runs the duchy; [[NPCs/Marek Thorne\|Marek Thorne]] went off as a mercenary and has only just returned; Calen himself was sent to a monastery and has not seen his brothers in years. He will put his own claim forward to the ducal throne and does not want to kill either brother. Asked about [[NPCs/Clara\|Clara]], he said that is a conversation he needs to have with her
- **The Order's pledge.** [[Party Members/Tooo-Ah\|Tooo-Ah]] asked Ser Alric to have the Silver Lanterns back Calen, but fumbled the persuasion (natural 1). Tessell stepped in, the artefacts he now carried lending his words weight, and Alric agreed to lead the Order with the party to restore the duchy to Calen, turning to Tessell: _"You guide the way, we will follow."_
- **The tavern night.** In the battered tavern, Ser Alric broke out a magical special ale that tastes of the drinker's favourite flavour, followed by a feast. Each drink or plate meant a d20 roll on Ant's effect table (most effects lasting 24 hours) - see Tavern Effects below. [[Party Members/Elik'r Stormswallow\|Elik'r Stormswallow]] led the party's karaoke reply to the next table with a natural 20 spoken-word ballad, "The Ballad of the First Lantern"
- **Guy 2.** [[Party Members/Elik'r Stormswallow\|Elik'r Stormswallow]] and [[Party Members/Elvva Beltavar\|Elvva Beltavar]] outlasted everyone, convinced each other that the kingdom's ducks are government spies, and blacked out. They woke clothed in the same room with a baby goat asleep between them; the innkeeper reported that they had come back loudly declaring it their child. Elik'r named it **Guy 2** after his late squire, and Ant made it canon as a sidekick both can command - see [[NPCs/Guy 2\|Guy 2]]. That morning the patrons hailed Elvva as _"Her Excellency Lady Wobblebottom the Tankard Conqueror, Lady of the Spilled Ale and Breaker of Barstools"_
- **Long rest.** The party took a long rest; Ant confirmed Tessell could attune to the glaive, and he did
- **Steve.** Elik'r tried to pass Pompaduke's message to Co'narr through the Steve stone and got no answer, so left a ten-second message: _"A man with a banjo says he will get you, you immortal bastard!"_ (adding that he now has a kid)
- **The march.** The party set off after [[NPCs/High Luminary Aelthir Vaelorian\|High Luminary Aelthir Vaelorian]]'s delegation towards [[Locations/Neverwinter\|Neverwinter]] with the Order of the Silver Lantern marching alongside: **41 heavily armoured knights and 63 acolytes and squires (104 in all)**, not all of them mounted. The party travelled with their own mounts and Calen's cart. Ant asked for d20 and d12 rolls for the journey; the results were not revealed before the session ended
