## Key Events

- The party woke at [[NPCs/Calen Thorne\|Calen Thorne]]'s hovel; [[Party Members/Ser Tessell Highfrost\|Ser Tessell Highfrost]] performed a 30-minute worship song, granting each party member who stayed within 20 feet for the entire duration their own stored boon (advantage on one ability check, saving throw, or attack roll, lasting until the following dawn)
- [[Party Members/Tooo-Ah\|Tooo-Ah]] and [[NPCs/Avicia\|Avicia]] had already left the hovel overnight; their whereabouts and the location of the party's squires were unknown for the rest of the session
- The party interrogated [[NPCs/Calen Thorne\|Calen Thorne]] further over breakfast, presenting him with three options for securing the Ducal throne of [[Locations/Neverwinter\|Neverwinter]]: an arranged marriage, winning the town's backing for an election, or removing his brothers ([[NPCs/Edran Thorne\|Edran Thorne]] and [[NPCs/Marek Thorne\|Marek Thorne]]) by less savoury means; Calen was visibly uncomfortable with anything beyond honourable battle
- The party negotiated Calen's recruitment: Tessell supplied the troop count (732), Odonia and Elik'r listed knights, swordsmen, and training facilities, Elik'r interjected with "we've got a herbalist," and Elvva offered up her (dead) pet frog - rather undercutting the attempt to sound impressive; Tessell separately bluffed his way through a claim of battle credentials, with the DM rolling a natural 1 for Calen's attempt to see through it
- Tessell used his own stored boon to persuade Calen to fetch sausages, which inadvertently triggered the next plot beat: Calen rushed back reporting the town was empty
- The party found [[Locations/Thalas'Miriel\|Thalas'Miriel]] eerily deserted, with the [[Organisations/Order of the Silver Lantern\|Order of the Silver Lantern]] garrison barricaded shut and signs of a recent skirmish (blood, dropped bolts and arrows) in the town square
- [[NPCs/Ser Alric Vayne\|Ser Alric Vayne]], now apparently in command of the garrison, explained that the town had been overrun by fast-moving, decayed, bewitched assailants - reported by a frightened guard as "zombies" - which had been driven back towards the [[Locations/Cathedral of Gentle Redemption\|Cathedral of Gentle Redemption]]
- The party (with Calen in tow) went to investigate, were ambushed by a wave of zombies outside the town's inn, and discovered [[NPCs/Lord Pompaduke the Third\|Lord Pompaduke the Third]] ("Banjo Man"/"Brother Algrim") directing the attack from a nearby rooftop while playing his lute
- After a long, multi-wave battle, Tessell silenced Pompaduke with the Silence spell, neutralising his magical abilities, while the party and Calen whittled down successive waves of zombies
- The session ended on a cliffhanger as reinforcements arrived down the lane: [[NPCs/Ser Alric Vayne\|Ser Alric Vayne]] leading Silver Lantern backup, with [[Party Members/Tooo-Ah\|Tooo-Ah]] in tow, just as the fight against Pompaduke and the zombies was still ongoing

## NPCs Encountered

- [[NPCs/Calen Thorne\|Calen Thorne]] - Joined the party on their journey after a chaotic negotiation; fought alongside them against the zombies, taking heavy damage but holding his own with sword and shield
- [[NPCs/Ser Alric Vayne\|Ser Alric Vayne]] - Found in command of the Silver Lantern garrison; briefed the party on the zombie incursion and the retreat to the cathedral; later led reinforcements to the party's aid
- [[NPCs/Lord Pompaduke the Third\|Lord Pompaduke the Third]] - Revealed as the figure directing the zombie attack from a rooftop, playing his lute ("boss music"); cast Counterspell and Hunger of Hadar-style spores; ultimately silenced and blinded by the party before reinforcements arrived

## Memorable Moments

- "I am Ser Tessell Highfrost, Commander of the Riders of the Storm, General of the Left" - delivered as a bluff about his battle credentials; the DM rolled a natural 1 for Calen's attempt to see through it, leaving Calen thoroughly convinced
- The "sausage questline": Tessell spent his own stored boon to persuade Calen to fetch sausages, only for Calen to return moments later reporting the town's disappearance - and for Tessell to get his silver back
- The party's negotiation with Calen spiralling from troop counts and knights into "we've got a herbalist" and a long-dead pet frog, undercutting her own attempt to sound impressive
- Elvva's Fireball being countered by Pompaduke, then counterspelled back through her own Counterspell, landing 42 damage on him in one exchange
- Tessell's Silence spell shutting down Pompaduke's spellcasting, followed by Elik'r's Warding Flame blinding him in quick succession - between the two, nearly everything the antagonist could do was shut down temporarily.
