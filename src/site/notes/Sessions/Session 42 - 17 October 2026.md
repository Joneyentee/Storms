**Location:** [[Locations/Thalas'Miriel Cemetery\|Thalas'Miriel Cemetery]] - the old mausoleum and the graves around it **Date In-Game:** Continues directly from Session 41 - evening of the same day; the whole session covers roughly four rounds of a single combat

## Key Events

- The DM's recap added detail on the figures met at the mausoleum in Session 41: the [[NPCs/Mysterious Cloaked Man\|Mysterious Cloaked Man]] (ornate cane) is the man [[Party Members/Tooo-Ah\|Tooo-Ah]] believes to be [[NPCs/Professor Gabriel Emms\|Professor Gabriel Emms]], the one casting the incantation when [[NPCs/Avicia\|Avicia]] was transformed from a doll, and the one present in the [[Locations/Belendar\|Belendar]] town square; the [[NPCs/Ember Vein Envoy - Unnamed\|Ember Vein Envoy - Unnamed]] was also present at Avicia's transformation, silent until she said "it is done now" and vanished; the [[NPCs/Unknown Axe Fighter\|Unknown Axe Fighter]] is the intruder [[Party Members/Ser Tessell Highfrost\|Ser Tessell Highfrost]] once caught rifling through [[NPCs/Countess Alyssa Crowsbane\|Countess Alyssa Crowsbane]]'s bedroom, who then jumped out through a window - not scared off, but apparently finished with his search
- The [[NPCs/Unknown Axe Fighter\|Unknown Axe Fighter]] was described in full for the first time: about 7'5", never seen out of full ebony plate with a slit-visored helm, carrying a massive executioner's axe
- He opened the fight by using [[NPCs/The Bane\|The Bane]] to burn a green rune into the ground - every Bane Watcher felt it pull at them - and spoke a summons: "Arise, the Veiled Oracle, the Hollow Blade, the Oathless, the Last Huntress." Stone broke inside the mausoleum and four skeletal champions emerged
- The Veiled Oracle cast a 4th-level Fireball across the party for 32 fire damage (halved for [[Party Members/Tooo-Ah\|Tooo-Ah]] and [[Party Members/Elik'r Stormswallow\|Elik'r Stormswallow]]), leaving most of the party bloodied in the first seconds; the rune visibly drained life from her as she cast
- The Oathless - a skeletal paladin in dark plate with a heavy mace, the lantern emblem on his armour deliberately scraped away with a blade - advanced on the party; [[Party Members/Tooo-Ah\|Tooo-Ah]] dropped out of the air onto his skull with her quarterstaff, landing Stunning Strike and 32 damage in one turn
- The Hollow Blade (chainmail, old two-handed greatsword) let out a Bane-charged screech; [[Party Members/Tooo-Ah\|Tooo-Ah]] alone failed the Wisdom save and, on her next turn, was compelled to attack [[Party Members/Lady Odonia of Galantri\|Lady Odonia of Galantri]] - missing twice
- The Last Huntress (white-haired skeletal archer) put two necrotic arrows into [[Party Members/Elvva Beltavar\|Elvva Beltavar]], dropping her to 1 HP
- [[Party Members/Elik'r Stormswallow\|Elik'r Stormswallow]] healed Elvva and planted the Stormswallow family banner for the first time: enemies within 30 feet lose 10 feet of speed, allies have advantage against frightened or charmed enemies, and chosen allies gain 3 temporary hit points every turn
- [[Party Members/Elvva Beltavar\|Elvva Beltavar]] answered with a Fireball that turned Bane-green as it left her hand - 53 damage including her Hex die, incinerating the Veiled Oracle outright and badly charring the others; she declined to gamble it on her [[Items/Coin of Fate\|Coin of Fate]]
- [[NPCs/Calen Thorne\|Calen Thorne]] revealed a new ability, Brace - setting himself behind [[Party Members/Ser Tessell Highfrost\|Ser Tessell Highfrost]] shield-wall style for +2 AC
- [[Party Members/Ser Tessell Highfrost\|Ser Tessell Highfrost]] used Turn Undead and Mass Healing Word (15 HP each) to pull the party back from the brink
- The [[NPCs/Unknown Axe Fighter\|Unknown Axe Fighter]] slammed his axe haft into the rune, tore 10 HP of life force each from [[Party Members/Tooo-Ah\|Tooo-Ah]], [[Party Members/Ser Tessell Highfrost\|Ser Tessell Highfrost]] and [[NPCs/Calen Thorne\|Calen Thorne]] ([[Party Members/Elik'r Stormswallow\|Elik'r Stormswallow]] resisted on a natural 20), and fired it as a green beam into the dead white tree, which came alive and began lashing out with its branches
- The Hollow Blade drove his greatsword into the ground, erupting noxious gas for 28 necrotic damage: [[Party Members/Ser Tessell Highfrost\|Ser Tessell Highfrost]] was knocked unconscious and [[Party Members/Elik'r Stormswallow\|Elik'r Stormswallow]] (natural 1) floored; Elik'r got Tessell back up with a Cure Wounds for 18 HP and cast Shield of Faith on Elvva
- [[Party Members/Ser Tessell Highfrost\|Ser Tessell Highfrost]] cast Aid on himself, [[Party Members/Tooo-Ah\|Tooo-Ah]] and [[NPCs/Calen Thorne\|Calen Thorne]] (+5 maximum HP for 8 hours), then summoned a Spiritual Weapon (a sabre) that cut the Last Huntress in half
- [[Party Members/Lady Odonia of Galantri\|Lady Odonia of Galantri]]'s [[Items/Infinite Radiant Bow\|Infinite Radiant Bow]] (double damage against these undead) destroyed the Hollow Blade with a shot under the chin, then the stunned Oathless with a shot to the head - he detonated in a necrotic blast that caught Elik'r and Tessell
- With his champions gone, the [[NPCs/Unknown Axe Fighter\|Unknown Axe Fighter]] stepped in - "You worms" - and struck [[Party Members/Elik'r Stormswallow\|Elik'r Stormswallow]] three times (to-hit rolls of 28, 24 and 22) for 43 damage, dropping him; he then turned on Tessell, who made the Wisdom save by a single point thanks to Elik'r's aura - failure would have petrified him
- A zombie horde of elves, half-elves and humans closed in from every edge of the cemetery, surrounding [[Party Members/Elvva Beltavar\|Elvva Beltavar]]; the banner slowed them to a crawl
- [[Party Members/Tooo-Ah\|Tooo-Ah]] failed a Medicine check on Elik'r; Elik'r passed his first death save, only for the DM to call in the disadvantage token and turn it into a failure; [[Party Members/Elvva Beltavar\|Elvva Beltavar]] stabilised him with Spare the Dying and Misty Stepped clear
- [[Party Members/Ser Tessell Highfrost\|Ser Tessell Highfrost]] cast Command on the [[NPCs/Unknown Axe Fighter\|Unknown Axe Fighter]] - "Retreat". After sneering "Stay dead, worm!" at Elik'r and "You're so pitiful, you're not worth my time", he vanished in black-purple flame; the DM confirmed he left only because he failed that save
- [[Party Members/Lady Odonia of Galantri\|Lady Odonia of Galantri]] stabbed the animated tree with the [[Items/Twin Blades of the Serpent\|Twin Blades of the Serpent]], killing it - but the stolen life force lashed back into Tessell, Tooo-Ah and Calen for another 10 necrotic damage each
- The party then broke the horde: [[Party Members/Elik'r Stormswallow\|Elik'r Stormswallow]]'s fire breath, [[Party Members/Elvva Beltavar\|Elvva Beltavar]]'s Aganazzar's Scorcher (a whole line of zombies destroyed), [[NPCs/Calen Thorne\|Calen Thorne]] breathing a cone of Elvva's Dragon's Breath (five more), and [[Party Members/Ser Tessell Highfrost\|Ser Tessell Highfrost]]'s second Turn Undead, backed by his hero token so that every zombie in range failed and fled
- [[Party Members/Lady Odonia of Galantri\|Lady Odonia of Galantri]], unable to attack without breaking the turn, cast a 2nd-level Cure Wounds on Tessell for 31 HP, taking him to full
- As the zombies scattered, the whole party made a Wisdom save; [[Party Members/Elik'r Stormswallow\|Elik'r Stormswallow]] (natural 1) and [[Party Members/Lady Odonia of Galantri\|Lady Odonia of Galantri]] each then rolled a d6 for an effect not yet revealed - and a lute began to play. [[NPCs/Lord Pompaduke the Third\|Lord Pompaduke the Third]] had only appeared to portal away in Session 41: he had not gone far and had been invisible near the mausoleum for the entire battle, the same trick he used on the rooftops in [[Locations/Thalas'Miriel\|Thalas'Miriel]]
