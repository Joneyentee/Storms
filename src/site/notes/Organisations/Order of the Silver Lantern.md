## Core Information

**Purpose:** Military arm of the [[Locations/Cathedral of Gentle Redemption\|Cathedral of Gentle Redemption]]; responsible for protecting the cathedral, enforcing its authority, and projecting military power from [[Locations/Thalas'Miriel\|Thalas'Miriel]]

**Alignment:** Politically aligned with the Falcon faction via [[NPCs/High Luminary Aelthir Vaelorian\|High Luminary Aelthir Vaelorian]]; may not represent all members' personal convictions (the young blonde monk's dissatisfaction with Vaelorian's politics suggests dissent exists within the broader cathedral community)

**Symbol:** Lantern crest (on armour)

**Uniform:** Blue plate armour with lantern crest

**Base:** Garrison within [[Locations/Thalas'Miriel\|Thalas'Miriel]], adjacent to or near the [[Locations/Cathedral of Gentle Redemption\|Cathedral of Gentle Redemption]]; includes a smithy

**Relationship to Cathedral:** Operates under the authority of [[NPCs/High Luminary Aelthir Vaelorian\|High Luminary Aelthir Vaelorian]]; the Order's military capability is an extension of the cathedral's political power

## History & Development

**Founding:** Founded by [[NPCs/Ser Elenai\|Ser Elenai]], the **First Lantern**, who carried her glaive "through the long nights" (the Long Night) and was entombed beneath the cemetery mausoleum outside [[Locations/Thalas'Miriel\|Thalas'Miriel]] with five trials of the Order's virtues - Vigilance, Mercy, Courage, Truth and Sacrifice. Her glaive's moonstone core is said to be a shard of the very first lantern lit to guide mortals from shadow. Her words on the Order's heart: a lantern is worthy not for the brightness of its light but for "the darkness it is willing to enter so that another might find their way"; those who "rise, and when another has fallen, do not leave them there" are what the Silver Lanterns stand for. The lantern crest also suggests a long-standing connection to the [[Locations/Cathedral of Gentle Redemption\|Cathedral of Gentle Redemption]] and its dedication to Ilmater.

**Pre-Session 36:** Order presumably existed as cathedral guardians and town enforcers throughout [[Locations/Thalas'Miriel\|Thalas'Miriel]]'s history. Confirmed Falcon-aligned by association with the cathedral (Session 28 briefing from [[NPCs/Ser Silvar Glinnan\|Ser Silvar Glinnan]]). Originally founded as monks who went abroad on crusade and returned committed to protecting and healing people - functioned similarly to crusader military orders. Historically open to the public.

**Session 36:** First direct encounter with the party. Arrested [[Party Members/Tooo-Ah\|Tooo-Ah]] after failed theft from cathedral collection basket; escorted to garrison. [[Party Members/Lady Odonia of Galantri\|Lady Odonia of Galantri]] visited smithy separately, examined three magic longswords.

**Session 37:** Garrison infiltrated by [[Organisations/Riders of the Storm\|Riders of the Storm]] via back waterway entrance. [[NPCs/Darius Kain\|Darius Kain]] confirmed as High Commander and cult-aligned. [[NPCs/Ser Alric Vayne\|Ser Alric Vayne]] (former second in command, imprisoned ~3 weeks for siding with Crown) freed alongside approximately 8 imprisoned Crown-loyal members. [[Party Members/Tooo-Ah\|Tooo-Ah]] rescued. Silver Lantern banners found defaced with green cult symbol. Party discovered hidden passage below garrison activated by [[NPCs/The Bane\|The Bane]] flowing from [[Party Members/Ser Tessell Highfrost\|Ser Tessell Highfrost]]'s palm - sealed inside at session end.

**Session 38:** [[NPCs/Darius Kain\|Darius Kain]] killed by [[Party Members/Tooo-Ah\|Tooo-Ah]] in the ritual chamber of the [[Locations/Ember Vein Underground Complex\|Ember Vein Underground Complex]]. Confirmed as [[Organisations/Ember Vein\|Ember Vein]] Third Vein operative. The Order's role as surface apparatus for the cult is ended at local command level. [[NPCs/Ser Alric Vayne\|Ser Alric Vayne]] and freed Crown loyalists holding the three-staircase junction below the garrison as rearguard.

**Session 43:** The founder's tomb found and its trials completed; [[Party Members/Ser Tessell Highfrost\|Ser Tessell Highfrost]] granted the First Lantern's glaive and recognised as the "First Light". The Order, under [[NPCs/Ser Alric Vayne\|Ser Alric Vayne]], committed 104 of its number to march with the party in support of [[NPCs/Calen Thorne\|Calen Thorne]]'s claim to [[Locations/Neverwinter\|Neverwinter]].
