### Description

An old, overgrown cemetery hidden within thick woodland south-west of [[Locations/Thalas'Miriel\|Thalas'Miriel]], reached via a crumbling, overgrown cobblestone path. The site is wrapped in unnatural magical darkness that thickens the deeper the party travels into it, and is confirmed as the source of the zombie horde that struck [[Locations/Thalas'Miriel\|Thalas'Miriel]] across Sessions 40-41.

### Geography

Situated within thick woodland roughly under 2 miles south-west of [[Locations/Thalas'Miriel\|Thalas'Miriel]] - close enough for a 10-minute walk or a 5-minute ride, but the undergrowth is too dense for horses, elk, or carts to pass, forcing the party to dismount and continue on foot. An old, crooked iron gate marks the entrance, flanked by two stone grotesque statues (not living creatures) overhead. Beyond the gate, a withered, dead white tree stands near an old mausoleum, surrounded by ancient graves. The area is affected by a magical darkness that intensifies with depth into the site and recedes when the party steps back.

### Key Features

- Overgrown cobblestone path leading in from the woodland edge
- Old, crooked iron gate with two stone grotesque statues
- Withered, dead white tree - briefly animated with stolen life force (S42), now inert again
- Old mausoleum - enclosed and roofed, about two storeys high, with tombs inside; four undead champions were raised from it (S42). **Its inner stone door bears the [[Organisations/Order of the Silver Lantern\|Order of the Silver Lantern]]'s lantern heraldry and opens only to prayer; behind it a staircase leads down to the [[Locations/Tomb of Ser Elenai\|Tomb of Ser Elenai]] (S43)**
- Numerous old graves - the zombie horde rose from all around the site (S42)
- A green rune of [[NPCs/The Bane\|The Bane]] burned into the ground in front of the mausoleum by the [[NPCs/Unknown Axe Fighter\|Unknown Axe Fighter]] (S42)

### Population

None - abandoned, though used clandestinely as a meeting site by Bane-aligned figures.

### Leadership

- No formal leadership; used as a gathering point by [[NPCs/Ember Vein Envoy - Unnamed\|Ember Vein Envoy - Unnamed]], [[NPCs/Lord Pompaduke the Third\|Lord Pompaduke the Third]], [[NPCs/Mysterious Cloaked Man\|Mysterious Cloaked Man]], and the [[NPCs/Unknown Axe Fighter\|Unknown Axe Fighter]]

### History

First glimpsed by [[Party Members/Tooo-Ah\|Tooo-Ah]] as a green, Bane-tinged haze while flying to join the [[Locations/Thalas'Miriel\|Thalas'Miriel]] battle at the end of Session 40. Confirmed this session as the source of the undead horde that had been attacking the town.

### Current Situation

The party found four Bane-aligned figures in a clandestine meeting at the mausoleum. Three departed after a confrontation - [[NPCs/Ember Vein Envoy - Unnamed\|Ember Vein Envoy - Unnamed]] revealing the location of "the other dragon" (Dragonclaw Isle) before leaving for Neverwinter, [[NPCs/Lord Pompaduke the Third\|Lord Pompaduke the Third]] portalling away, and [[NPCs/Mysterious Cloaked Man\|Mysterious Cloaked Man]] casting a necromancy spell before vanishing. The [[NPCs/Unknown Axe Fighter\|Unknown Axe Fighter]] remained and challenged the party to combat.

**Session 42:** The Axe Fighter burned a Bane rune into the ground and raised four undead champions from the mausoleum (see [[NPCs/Champions of the Mausoleum\|Champions of the Mausoleum]]), then drained life force from three of the party to animate the dead white tree. The party destroyed all four champions and the tree; the Axe Fighter was forced to withdraw by [[Party Members/Ser Tessell Highfrost\|Ser Tessell Highfrost]]'s Command. A zombie horde of elves, half-elves and humans then closed in from every side and was turned by Tessell. As the zombies fled, [[NPCs/Lord Pompaduke the Third\|Lord Pompaduke the Third]] revealed he had been invisible beside the mausoleum throughout.

**Session 43:** Pompaduke threatened [[NPCs/Co'narr 'Banewatcher' Hyborian\|Co'narr 'Banewatcher' Hyborian]] through [[Party Members/Elik'r Stormswallow\|Elik'r Stormswallow]] and portalled away, collapsing the remaining skeletons with The Bane as he left. [[Party Members/Ser Tessell Highfrost\|Ser Tessell Highfrost]], guided by a goddess's voice, prayed open the mausoleum's lantern door and the party descended into the [[Locations/Tomb of Ser Elenai\|Tomb of Ser Elenai]], founder of the [[Organisations/Order of the Silver Lantern\|Order of the Silver Lantern]]. The zombies that fled towards [[Locations/Thalas'Miriel\|Thalas'Miriel]] were found crumbled to dust at the gates.
