### Description

An old, overgrown cemetery hidden within thick woodland south-west of [[Locations/Thalas'Miriel\|Thalas'Miriel]], reached via a crumbling, overgrown cobblestone path. The site is wrapped in unnatural magical darkness that thickens the deeper the party travels into it, and is confirmed as the source of the zombie horde that struck [[Locations/Thalas'Miriel\|Thalas'Miriel]] across Sessions 40-41.

### Geography

Situated within thick woodland roughly under 2 miles south-west of [[Locations/Thalas'Miriel\|Thalas'Miriel]] - close enough for a 10-minute walk or a 5-minute ride, but the undergrowth is too dense for horses, elk, or carts to pass, forcing the party to dismount and continue on foot. An old, crooked iron gate marks the entrance, flanked by two stone grotesque statues (not living creatures) overhead. Beyond the gate, a withered, dead white tree stands near an old mausoleum, surrounded by ancient graves. The area is affected by a magical darkness that intensifies with depth into the site and recedes when the party steps back.

### History

First glimpsed by [[Party Members/Tooo-Ah\|Tooo-Ah]] as a green, Bane-tinged haze while flying to join the [[Locations/Thalas'Miriel\|Thalas'Miriel]] battle at the end of Session 40. Confirmed this session as the source of the undead horde that had been attacking the town.

### Current Situation

The party found four Bane-aligned figures in a clandestine meeting at the mausoleum. Three departed after a confrontation - [[NPCs/Ember Vein Envoy - Unnamed\|Ember Vein Envoy - Unnamed]] revealing the location of "the other dragon" (Dragonclaw Isle) before leaving for Neverwinter, [[NPCs/Lord Pompaduke the Third\|Lord Pompaduke the Third]] portalling away, and [[NPCs/Mysterious Cloaked Man\|Mysterious Cloaked Man]] casting a necromancy spell before vanishing. The [[NPCs/Unknown Axe Fighter\|Unknown Axe Fighter]] remained and challenged the party to combat - session ended on the initiative roll, confrontation unresolved.
