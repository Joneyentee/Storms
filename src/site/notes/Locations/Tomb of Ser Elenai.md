### Description

The resting place of [[NPCs/Ser Elenai\|Ser Elenai]], the First Lantern and founder of the [[Organisations/Order of the Silver Lantern\|Order of the Silver Lantern]]. The mausoleum above is only an entrance: its stone door bears the Order's lantern heraldry and opened to [[Party Members/Ser Tessell Highfrost\|Ser Tessell Highfrost]]'s prayer and knock, revealing a staircase down. Below lies a vast circular, barrel-vaulted chamber, undisturbed for centuries, holding the founder's statue and five trial statues.

### Geography

Reached by a stairwell below the cemetery mausoleum; the doors grind shut once the party is inside. The chamber is tall enough for an aarakocra to fly freely and is lit by light glowing through cracks in the ceiling. Old decor has weathered and ivy has grown in. Above the first statue, stars are carved into the ceiling.

### Key Features

- **Central statue** - a regal female knight in ceremonial armour and spiked crown holding an enormous lantern-headed glaive; an inscription beneath (the prophecy). Turned to face Tessell when he prayed. **Crumbled once the glaive was claimed (S43)**
- **Sarcophagus** - a drawer-like tomb in the base of the central statue, holding [[NPCs/Ser Elenai\|Ser Elenai]]'s body in the Order's ceremonial armour; opened at the end of the trials
- **Encased lantern** on a plinth before the central statue - once lit, long dark; the lights of the trials were directed into it, and it spoke to the party
- **Five trial statues** around the outer wall, each with a smooth pressure plate in front and a school of magic long faded from it:

### The Prophecy

Inscribed beneath the central statue and recited to the party by the founder's voice:

> _When silver fades and ancient stones laid bare,_
> _a stranger born of two worlds shall enter there._
> _A son of Winter, walking beneath another sky,_
> _whose greatest victories are oft preceded by a fall._
> _He shall charge where wiser souls would hesitate_
> _and find himself upon the ground before the gate._
> _He shall steal the hearts of noble blood,_
> _though some may seek to spill his own._
> _A countess shall offer him affection sweet,_
> _while sharpening the blade beneath her seat._
> _Yet mock him not, for beneath the fool's disguise,_
> _a stubborn flame within him never dies._
> _For when the helpless cry and the darkness draws near,_
> _he shall stand where others yield to fear._
> _Five lights shall judge the hearts he bears,_
> _and one shall answer all his prayers._
> _Not the strongest hand nor the purest soul,_
> _but the one that helps another become whole._
> _The First Lantern shall surrender her flame,_
> _and the world may yet remember Tessell's name._

**DM's summary (S43):** centuries ago it was foretold that someone called Tessell - with poetic licence over the name "Highfrost" - would overcome this trial by completing five different tasks with the help of others.
