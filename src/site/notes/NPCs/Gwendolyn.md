### Description

An elderly woman who appears among the guests and contenders at the Belendar tournament festivities. Despite her age she takes part in the chariot racing and the joust, and she is a surprisingly cheerful companion to [[NPCs/Co'narr 'Banewatcher' Hyborian\|Co'narr 'Banewatcher' Hyborian]], who is otherwise gruff and unsociable.

### Key Details

- Paired with [[Party Members/Elik'r Stormswallow\|Elik'r Stormswallow]] for the chariot race in Session 12 (per Kieran's notes)
- Helped [[NPCs/Co'narr 'Banewatcher' Hyborian\|Co'narr 'Banewatcher' Hyborian]] attack the hooded figures in Session 13 (per Kieran's notes)
- Entered the joust (Session 14, per Kieran's notes)
- At the Session 21 feast she entered arm in arm with Co'narr, who was wearing proper clothes for the first time, and he escorted her to another table and sat beside her
- In the Session 21 joust she was unhorsed by [[NPCs/Tharrock the Unyielding\|Tharrock the Unyielding]] on the first tilt

### DM Notes

- Inspired by a real old woman who struck up a conversation with Ant on the bus while he was doing D&D prep. Ant later confirmed this at the table during Session 24 ("the woman I sat on the bus").
- Pure colour NPC: she fills out the tournament field and gives Co'narr an unexpected foil.
