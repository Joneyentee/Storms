### Description

Four skeletal champions raised from the old mausoleum in the [[Locations/Thalas'Miriel Cemetery\|Thalas'Miriel Cemetery]] by the [[NPCs/Unknown Axe Fighter\|Unknown Axe Fighter]], who called them by title: "Arise, the Veiled Oracle, the Hollow Blade, the Oathless, the Last Huntress." Each fought as a distinct warrior of an earlier age, drawing power from a green rune of [[NPCs/The Bane\|The Bane]] burned into the ground - a rune that visibly drained them as they used it. Who they were in life is unknown. All four were destroyed by the [[Organisations/Riders of the Storm\|Riders of the Storm]] in the same battle.

### Key Details

- **The Veiled Oracle** - veiled skeletal spellcaster; cast a 4th-level Fireball for 32 fire damage across the party; the rune drained an ether-like essence from her as she cast. Incinerated by [[Party Members/Elvva Beltavar\|Elvva Beltavar]]'s Bane-green Fireball
- **The Hollow Blade** - skeleton in old chainmail and hardened leather with a two-handed greatsword; let out a Bane-charged screech (Wisdom save or be compelled to attack an ally on the next turn) and drove his sword into the ground to erupt noxious gas for 28 necrotic damage (Dexterity save). Destroyed by [[Party Members/Lady Odonia of Galantri\|Lady Odonia of Galantri]]'s Infinite Radiant Bow
- **The Oathless** - skeletal paladin in dark plate with a heavy mace; the **lantern emblem on his armour had been deliberately scraped away with a blade**, suggesting a fallen knight of the [[Organisations/Order of the Silver Lantern\|Order of the Silver Lantern]]. Stunned by [[Party Members/Tooo-Ah\|Tooo-Ah]] for most of the fight; exploded in a necrotic blast (13 damage, Dexterity save for half) when destroyed by [[Party Members/Lady Odonia of Galantri\|Lady Odonia of Galantri]]
- **The Last Huntress** - white-haired skeletal archer with a longbow and short sword; fired two necrotic arrows a turn, taking [[Party Members/Elvva Beltavar\|Elvva Beltavar]] to 1 HP. Cut in half by [[Party Members/Ser Tessell Highfrost\|Ser Tessell Highfrost]]'s Spiritual Weapon
- Radiant damage from the Infinite Radiant Bow was doubled against them
- Which title belongs to which figure was only stated outright for the Veiled Oracle; the other three are matched by description
