### Description

A massive purple-scaled dragonborn standing approximately twelve feet tall, armoured in heavy plate. Confirmed in S39 as a direct ancestor of [[Party Members/Elik'r Stormswallow\|Elik'r Stormswallow]]'s bloodline — the progenitor who bound a wyrm beneath the world at the cost of his own life, and was entombed in stone as a consequence. Fought the party on awakening, believing them to have broken his seal. Defeated in a two-phase boss fight (S39); in his final moments passed his longsword to [[Party Members/Elik'r Stormswallow\|Elik'r Stormswallow]] and gestured to the Stormswallow family banner. His body crumbled to stone dust. His name was never stated.

### Key Details

- Height: 12 feet tall; purple scales (non-standard, homebrew)
- Carried a longsword with a dark iron blade shot through with ember-red veins of light; passed to [[Party Members/Elik'r Stormswallow\|Elik'r Stormswallow]] on death (resized to fit); properties unidentified
- **[[Party Members/Elik'r Stormswallow\|Elik'r Stormswallow]]'s family crest** present on a clasp on his armour — confirmed by Elik'r on sight (S38)
- Sacrificed himself in ancient history to bind a wyrm beneath the world; was petrified as a result; name burned into stone (his words) so the seal would hold
- In Elik'r's Bane Watcher initiation vision (S18), screamed directly at him by name: _"RELEASE ME, RELEASE ME, ELIK'R STORMSWALLOW!"_
- On awakening (S39), addressed [[Party Members/Elik'r Stormswallow\|Elik'r Stormswallow]] directly: _"You carry my line. And yet you undo my sacrifice."_
- Resistant to fire and bludgeoning damage; vulnerable to thunder and lightning
- Special ability: Blazing Step (half-speed movement, 4 fire damage to adjacent creatures, no opportunity attacks)
- Dying words to Elik'r: _"Do what I could not do, for the sake of our family in the world"_; gestured to the Stormswallow family banner as _"a symbol of what our job is, what our family's right is to be"_
- Name never revealed before death
