### Description

Ser Elenai, the **First Lantern**, founder of the [[Organisations/Order of the Silver Lantern\|Order of the Silver Lantern]]. A regal female knight, depicted in her tomb in ceremonial armour and a spiked crown, bearing an enormous glaive whose head is an open lantern frame around a glowing moonstone. She carried that light "through the long nights" - the item card names it the Long Night. No surname has been given. Her tomb lies below the old mausoleum in the [[Locations/Thalas'Miriel Cemetery\|Thalas'Miriel Cemetery]], undisturbed for centuries until the party opened it in Session 43.

### Key Details

- Founder of the [[Organisations/Order of the Silver Lantern\|Order of the Silver Lantern]]; known as the First Lantern
- Wielded [[Items/The First Lantern's Glaive\|The First Lantern's Glaive]], said to hold a shard of the very first lantern lit to guide mortals from shadow
- Her tomb holds five trials - Vigilance, Mercy, Courage, Truth and Sacrifice - tied to a prophecy naming [[Party Members/Ser Tessell Highfrost\|Ser Tessell Highfrost]] (full text in [[Locations/Tomb of Ser Elenai\|Tomb of Ser Elenai]])
- Her voice spoke to the party through her statue and directly to Tessell once the trials were complete; she had "watched" him, and knew of his castle faceplant and of [[NPCs/Countess Alyssa Crowsbane\|Countess Alyssa Crowsbane]]
- Entrusted her glaive to Tessell: _"The First Lantern's watch has just ended. Yours has just begun."_
- Her central statue crumbled once the glaive was claimed; her body lies in the opened sarcophagus in the Order's ceremonial armour
- By the Order's leadership legends, the glaive is only ever given willingly, and the one who receives it takes on being the "First Light" ([[NPCs/Ser Alric Vayne\|Ser Alric Vayne]], S43)
