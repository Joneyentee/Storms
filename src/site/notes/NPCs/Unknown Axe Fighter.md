#### Description

Towering armoured figure, about 7'5" tall, never seen out of full ebony plate - black, ornate and more grandiose than any regular soldier's - with a full helm whose only opening is a narrow eye slit. Carries a massive two-handed axe, like an executioner's, which the party can sense is magical. First encountered by [[Party Members/Ser Tessell Highfrost\|Ser Tessell Highfrost]], who found him in [[NPCs/Countess Alyssa Crowsbane\|Countess Alyssa Crowsbane]]'s bedroom rifling through her drawers; he then jumped out through a window - not scared off by Tessell, but apparently having finished his search and leaving abruptly. Watched the Session 25 throne room duel without engaging. In Session 42 he proved to be a wielder of [[NPCs/The Bane\|The Bane]] as well as a devastating melee fighter. True identity and allegiance unknown; the DM's own notes list him only as "The Unknown".

#### Key Details

- Height: about 7'5" (confirmed by the DM)
- Full ebony plate with a slit-visored helm; no gap an archer can reach from the front (S42)
- Massive two-handed magical axe (party can sense magic)
- Found by [[Party Members/Ser Tessell Highfrost\|Ser Tessell Highfrost]] in [[NPCs/Countess Alyssa Crowsbane\|Countess Alyssa Crowsbane]]'s bedroom, rifling through her drawers; jumped out through a window once his search appeared finished, rather than being scared off
- Watched the [[NPCs/Thalas\|Thalas]] vs [[NPCs/Crown Princess Eilrora Everglade\|Crown Princess Eilrora Everglade]] duel without engaging (S25)
- **Wields The Bane (S42):** burned a green Bane rune into the ground at the mausoleum; every Bane Watcher present felt it pulling at them
- **Raises undead champions (S42):** summoned four with the words "Arise, the Veiled Oracle, the Hollow Blade, the Oathless, the Last Huntress" - see [[NPCs/Champions of the Mausoleum\|Champions of the Mausoleum]]
- **Life drain (S42):** struck his axe haft on the rune; Constitution save or lose 10 HP (three of four targets failed); gathered the stolen life force into a green beam and used it to animate a dead tree
- **Melee (S42):** three attacks in a turn - two axe blows and a strike with the haft - hitting on 28, 24 and 22 for 43 damage, dropping [[Party Members/Elik'r Stormswallow\|Elik'r Stormswallow]]
- **Petrification (S42):** forced a DC 16 Wisdom save on [[Party Members/Ser Tessell Highfrost\|Ser Tessell Highfrost]] that would have petrified him on a failure
- **Withdrawal (S42):** failed the save against Tessell's Command ("Retreat") and left through black-purple flames; the DM confirmed he left only because of the failed save
- Contemptuous of the party: "You worms", "Stay dead, worm!", "You're so pitiful, you're not worth my time"
