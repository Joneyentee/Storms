## Physical Description

Aarakocra (bird-like humanoid) monk. Can fly 50ft per round. Parrot or eagle-like features with feathered body, long narrow legs with sharp talons.

## Background

Formerly part of gang with [[NPCs/Logrin the Dwarf\|Logrin the Dwarf]]. Brews magical teas with random d100 effects. Known for terminal velocity experiments and flying into trees while hunting. Adopted [[NPCs/Avicia\|Avicia]] (animated doll brought to life).

**Military Rank:** Chariot General (Session 26 - appointed by [[NPCs/Crown Princess Eilrora Everglade\|Crown Princess Eilrora Everglade]])

## Character Development

### Major Moments

**Early Campaign (Session 1-2):**

- **Set Log Trap:** Party positioned logs/trees on either side of high-sided valley to ambush 100+ pursuing dwarves, Tooo-Ah stayed behind alone to trigger rolling logs onto enemy force below, then flew off to rejoin party
- **FOUND THE DOLL (Critical):** During side quest clearing smugglers from woods near [[Locations/Canathalas\|Canathalas]], discovered a doll in a chest on captured/ransacked boat, decided to keep it not knowing significance - **this doll was the transformed [[NPCs/Avicia\|Avicia]]**, the rightful heir to throne, protected for centuries
- **Capture & Escape Failure:** During voyage to [[Locations/Canathalas\|Canathalas]], flew over to investigate approaching ships to determine if friendly, completely forgot how to communicate, landed on their deck, was captured, somehow talked way out without learning anything useful, forcing party to change course east

**Grand Tournament at Belendar (Sessions ~12-14):**

- **Placed Highly in Dancing Competition:** Strong performance in dancing event, placing among top competitors

Attempted to Warg into the Frost Wolf during combat with female orc rider - met by rider's spirit in a green-lit spirit realm, punched back for 12 psychic damage. Morning tea compelled her to punch [[Party Members/Ser Tessell Highfrost\|Ser Tessell Highfrost]]. Caught stealing from the cathedral collection basket at the Cathedral of Gentle Redemption (Sleight of Hand 12, Deception 9 - failed); escorted to the [[Organisations/Order of the Silver Lantern\|Order of the Silver Lantern]] garrison by guards to end the session.
- **[[Sessions/Session 42 - 17 October 2026\|Session 42 - 17 October 2026]]:** Stunned the Oathless with Stunning Strike at the [[Locations/Thalas'Miriel Cemetery\|Thalas'Miriel Cemetery]] mausoleum (32 damage in one turn). Alone failed the Wisdom save against the Hollow Blade's Bane-charged screech and was compelled to attack [[Party Members/Lady Odonia of Galantri\|Lady Odonia of Galantri]] on her next turn - both attacks missed. Had 10 HP of life force torn from her by the [[NPCs/Unknown Axe Fighter\|Unknown Axe Fighter]] to animate a dead tree.
- **[[Sessions/Session 43 - 14 November 2026\|Session 43 - 14 November 2026]]:** Completed the Vigilance trial in the [[Locations/Tomb of Ser Elenai\|Tomb of Ser Elenai]] - flew up, counted 13 stars carved in the ceiling against 12 on the pedestal, and pressed the extra star. Asked [[NPCs/Ser Alric Vayne\|Ser Alric Vayne]] for the Order's backing of [[NPCs/Calen Thorne\|Calen Thorne]] (natural 1; Tessell finished the job). At the tavern a duck dinner left two dogs following her, and a slice of cake made everyone call her "Your Deliciousness".

### Character Arc

Monk with criminal past (gang member) now adventuring hero. Adopted [[NPCs/Avicia\|Avicia]] showing caring side - **enormous responsibility as adopted parent of rightful heir**. Known for comedic moments (terminal velocity experiments, tree collisions). Brewing magical teas as unique ability. **Appointed Chariot General Session 26** - military responsibility alongside parental duties.
