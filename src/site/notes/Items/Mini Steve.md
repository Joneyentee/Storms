### Description

A painted rock with a face - a parting gift from Co'narr when he left for [[Locations/Nolunia\|Nolunia]], and Elik'r's permanent backpack companion.

### Properties

**Not magical** - purely sentimental. Represents Elik'r's completed Bane Watcher training: "Steve" in the Dream Temple was a manifestation of Co'narr's training method.

### Notes

- Elik'r cut off his own pinky finger drunk-carving a potato girlfriend for Mini Steve (S34)
