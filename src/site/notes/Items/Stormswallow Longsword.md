### Description

Dark iron blade threaded with ember-red veins. Formerly wielded by the [[NPCs/Purple Dragonborn\|Purple Dragonborn]], a Stormswallow ancestor; it resized itself to Elik'r's proportions when it passed to him. Unnamed (working title).

### Properties

- Longsword (1d8 slashing, versatile 1d10)
- **+2 weapon** (added to damage rolls in S40; Ant called it an "upgraded sword")
- Grants the wielder **fire resistance** (S40, S42) and **necrotic resistance** (S42 - halved necrotic damage three times at the cemetery mausoleum)
- **Ember Wave** _(on a successful hit)_ - as part of the same action, slam the blade into the ground to unleash a 10-foot radius wave of fire; DC 14 Dexterity save for half damage
- Other properties not yet identified

### Notes

- *To verify with Ant:* a Session 40 damage roll reads as 1d12+2, and Ant said it is large enough to need two hands - both differ from the 1d8/versatile 1d10 above
