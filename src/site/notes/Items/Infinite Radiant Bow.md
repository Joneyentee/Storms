### Properties

- Standard longbow damage (1d8 + modifier)
- **+1d4 radiant damage** on every hit (confirmed S38)
- Infinite magical arrows - no ammunition to track and no physical arrows needed
- **Table rule (S42):** radiant damage is doubled against undead; as the bow always deals radiant damage, its hits were doubled against the undead champions at the [[Locations/Thalas'Miriel Cemetery\|Thalas'Miriel Cemetery]]. This applies to radiant damage from any source

### History

Kieran's notes (all pre-Session 18) record Odonia being offered either 15 armoured knights or a "unique longbow" for her investigation, and choosing the bow. The bow itself was presented in Session 21 (after a d4 roll to pick one of four bows) and delivered to her house after the ball.
