### Description

An indigo-violet silk banner edged in dark iron, embroidered with a massive purple dragon, wings spread, bound tight by thick rune-chains. Its eyes glow with violet lightning, and sparks leak from where the chains bite its scales; the fabric ripples as if the beast strains within. In play it was described hanging from a long gold and red wooden shaft. The dragonborn called it _"a symbol of what our job is, what our family's right is to be"_.

It deals no attacks: its power sustains allies and drags down foes. Effects apply within 30 feet when planted or held.

> _"He is bound. Not broken. And through his fury, we endure."_

### Properties

**Activation:** planted in the ground as a bonus action, or held; radius 30 feet.

**Base Powers (attuned):**

- **Bonded Resolve** - chosen allies within range gain temporary hit points equal to Elik'r's proficiency bonus (3) every turn, at the start of his turn
- **Aura of Subjugation** - allies have advantage on attacks against frightened or charmed enemies
- **Chain-Wind Hindrance** - enemies within range have their speed reduced by 10 feet

**Unlocked at Level 8:**

- **Lightning of the Captive** - allies deal +1d4 lightning damage on weapon attacks
- **Restraining Aura** - enemies cannot Disengage unless they pass a DC 13 Strength save
- **Release the Rush** _(1/short rest, bonus action)_ - allies gain +10 feet of speed and a bonus Dash

**Unlocked at Level 10:**

- **Ward of the Storm-Wyrm** - allies gain resistance to lightning and thunder damage
- **Dread of the Bound Beast** - enemies have disadvantage on attacks against anyone but the bearer
- **Roar of Confinement** _(1/day, action)_ - DC 14 Constitution save; on a fail, the target is deafened and its speed becomes 0

**Unlocked at Level 15:**

- **Ascendant Wyrm** - allies deal +1d6 lightning damage and have advantage on Strength checks and saves
- **Prison of Chains** - the area becomes difficult terrain; foes that teleport take 4d6 lightning damage (DC 15 Constitution save negates)
- **Leakage of Power** - each turn, three enemies take 2d6 lightning damage (no save)
- **Partial Unleashing** _(1/long rest, 1 minute)_ - allies are immune to lightning, thunder and restraint; enemies have disadvantage on everything

### Notes

- Elik'r chooses which allies benefit from Bonded Resolve
- Temporary hit points do not revive an unconscious ally (DM ruling, S42)
- Full item card supplied by Kieran (Nov 2026)
- The banner is a physical object standing in the fight; it may be possible for enemies to damage it
- The seraph's vision in S38 showed this banner burning
