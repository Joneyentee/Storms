### Description

Bright orange cloak, "more like a cape to attach onto your arm". Elik'r called it his "Mist Form" cloak in S33; named "the Ethereal Traveller" in S36.

### Properties

- **Misty Step** - once per day, cast as a 2nd-level spell without spending a spell slot
- **Ethereal Step** - bonus action, lasts 1 minute: phase partially into the Ethereal and move through creatures and objects as if they were difficult terrain
- Ant said in S40 that most of its abilities are bonus actions
- **Rulings (S42):** the cloak's Misty Step uses his action; Ethereal Step only lets him pass through creatures and objects when *he* moves - attacks against him are not affected

### Notes

- Taken with the party's other gear by Fizwidget at the start of S33 (Ant confirmed the cloak was included)
- **Why Elik'r got a box:** at the feast Fizwidget served the party rounds of magical drinks with random effects. Elik'r turned down his round-two drink, so Fizwidget gave him a choice of two presents instead (_"You don't want to be the odd one out, do you? How very boring"_). He picked the red one; Ant asked him to choose the cloak's colour and he said orange
- The other present held a massively long spear, which Fizwidget flung down the hall, where it vanished into a wall
- Not one of the Carnival Realm rewards (circlet, rope, wand, hide armour), which came at the end of the toadstool adventure
- Full item-card text not yet checked - there may be further abilities
