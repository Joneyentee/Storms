### Description

Looks like a sapphire to ordinary appraisers, but appears alive, with an eerie swirling light inside.

### Properties

**Properties unknown** - requires Identify, or a reveal from the DM.

### History

Formed when the Dream Temple's dragon shed a single tear after Elvva genuinely forgave [[Party Members/Elik'r Stormswallow\|Elik'r Stormswallow]] for her death. It is the **only item from the Dream Temple that persisted into reality** - every other dream gem (100+) vanished on waking. Re-examined in S41 without result.
