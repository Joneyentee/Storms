### Properties

- Two cursed rings that link their wearers psychically and physically: mental communication is possible, and **damage to one wearer also hurts the other** (shared HP loss)
- Range and other limitations unknown
- **The curse:** once worn, the only way to remove them is for the two wearers to kiss

### History

[[Party Members/Ser Tessell Highfrost\|Ser Tessell Highfrost]] and [[Party Members/Elik'r Stormswallow\|Elik'r Stormswallow]] put the rings on and found themselves linked. They slipped away from the palace party to remove them privately. Elik'r then offered his ring to [[NPCs/Ser Anfalen\|Ser Anfalen]], who rejected it with disdain.
