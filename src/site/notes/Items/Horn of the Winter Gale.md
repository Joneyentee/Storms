### Description

A long carved horn from a long-dead ice beast.

### Properties

Once per long rest (action), blow the horn to create a 30-foot cone of freezing wind:

- DC 15 Constitution save
- **Fail:** 4d8 cold damage and knocked prone
- **Success:** half damage, remain standing
- The area becomes icy terrain until the end of the turn
