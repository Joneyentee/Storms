### Description

Small gold key with a tiny green eye set in the bow.

### Properties

Once per long rest, insert into any locked door and turn, then roll 1d6:

- **1** - the door explodes
- **2-3** - remains stubbornly locked (no negative consequence)
- **4-5** - opens normally
- **6** - opens to a completely different location (DM's choice)

⚠️ Does not override doors sealed by the [[Items/Permanent Lockpicks\|Permanent Lockpicks]].
