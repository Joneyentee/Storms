### Description

A pair of shortswords with sleek, curved blades resembling the fangs of a snake, made of dark, near-obsidian metal with a faint greenish shimmer. The hilts are wrapped in black leather with serpent motifs etched into the metalwork. A hidden mechanism in each hilt houses a magical chain of interlocking scales that can emerge and connect the two swords. When connected, the chain can be adjusted in length, so the blades can be wielded together in a variety of combat styles.

### Properties

- **Serpent's Chain** _(bonus action)_ - the chain extends and connects the two blades, up to 10 feet. While connected, the swords can be used for special manoeuvres such as sweeping attacks or wrapping the chain around an enemy, including:
  - **Trip** - target makes a DC 15 Dexterity save or is tripped
  - **Disarm** - target makes a DC 15 Strength save or is disarmed
  - Retracted or extended again as a bonus action
- **Whirling Fang Strike** _(action, 1/short or long rest)_ - while connected, make a single attack roll; on a hit, it can target all creatures within a 10-foot radius, dealing 1d6 slashing damage to each
- **Venomous Bite** _(passive, on a critical hit)_ - target makes a DC 14 Constitution save or takes an extra 2d6 poison damage and is poisoned for 1 minute
