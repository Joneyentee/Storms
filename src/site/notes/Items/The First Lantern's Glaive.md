### Description

Wielded by [[NPCs/Ser Elenai\|Ser Elenai]], First Lantern and founder of the [[Organisations/Order of the Silver Lantern\|Order of the Silver Lantern]]. Its head is shaped like an open lantern frame enclosing a glowing moonstone core. It is a weapon that pushes back darkness and tends to the wounded.

### Properties

||One-handed|Two-handed|
|---|---|---|
|**Damage**|1d8 slashing|1d12 slashing|
|**Reach**|5 ft|10 ft|

Weight 6 lb; versatile.

### Attunement Benefits

- **Inner Lantern** - the moonstone sheds 20 ft of bright light and a further 20 ft of dim light; bonus action to suppress or resume. Magical darkness only halves its radius - it can never be fully extinguished
- **One-Handed Guard** - no penalty when wielded one-handed; the free hand may hold a shield, holy symbol or spellcasting focus. Allies within 10 ft have advantage on saves against being frightened
- **Two-Handed Beacon** - +1 to damage rolls. Once per turn on a hit, expend 1 Channel Divinity to cast Dispel Magic on that target, no action required

### Activated Abilities

- **Lantern's Sanctuary** _(1/day, recharges at dawn; action; requires two hands)_ - plant the haft: a 30 ft radius sphere of silver light bursts out for 1 minute. All magical darkness in range is dispelled; allies gain +1 AC and advantage against curses and being frightened; allies regain 1d4 + Wisdom modifier HP at the start of their turn within the aura; undead and fiends take 1d6 radiant damage if they start their turn inside
- **Warding Glow** _(2/day, recharges at dawn; reaction when you or an ally within 30 ft takes damage)_ - a silver flare grants resistance to that damage; if the attacker is undead or a fiend, it takes 1d6 radiant damage in return. Works in either hand

### History

Ser Elenai carried this glaive through the Long Night. Its moonstone core is said to be a shard from the very first lantern lit to guide mortals from shadow. By the legends passed down through the Order's leadership, it is never handed over unwillingly, and its bearer takes on being the **"First Light"** ([[NPCs/Ser Alric Vayne\|Ser Alric Vayne]], S43). After the party passed the five trials in her tomb, Ser Elenai's spirit entrusted it to Tessell and it flew to his hand.

**Order Motto (on the card):** _"Where this glaive stands, the dark shall not pass. Where its light falls, the wounded shall rise."_
