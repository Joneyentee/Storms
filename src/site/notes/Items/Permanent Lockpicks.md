### Properties

- Permanently locks anything (even objects without locks), creating a magical seal
- **Cannot unlock anything**
- No known method to break locks created; no save or check required
- Doors sealed with them cannot be opened by the [[Items/Key of the Wrong Door\|Key of the Wrong Door]]

⚠️ **Warning:** The effect is irreversible.
