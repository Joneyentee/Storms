### Description

A cursed longsword of iron bearing four glyphs; on study (S8) it showed evocation magic and a feeling of flames.

### Properties

- Longsword (1d8 slashing, versatile 1d10)
- Can be enhanced with Elik'r's dragon breath to add fire damage
- **Charge Mechanic** - charges by absorbing HP from Elik'r himself: he must deal damage to himself. Requires **100 HP total** to fully charge; progress indicator unknown
- **Activation (at 100 HP charged)** - a flaming cone attack, 4d10 + 2 damage (Kieran's early notes say 4d10+3, the fire slash Talas used - to confirm); cone dimensions unconfirmed

⚠️ **Warning:** At 46 HP maximum, Elik'r cannot fully charge it in one encounter. The full cost of the curse is not yet known.

### Notes

- Kieran also noted a "Necromancy School" version "renamed the Mortician", charged by the wielder's blood, which makes a fire sword slash (4d10+3)
