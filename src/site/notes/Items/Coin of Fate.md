### Description

Tarnished silver coin of unknown origin.

### Properties

Once per long rest, when Elvva hits a creature with an attack, she can flip the coin before damage is dealt:

- **Heads** - the target takes double damage
- **Tails** - Elvva takes the damage instead (at normal value)

### Notes

- The table talked her out of flipping it on her 53-damage Fireball in S42
