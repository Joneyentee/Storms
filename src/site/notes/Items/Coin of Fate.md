### Description

A tarnished silver coin of unknown origin that seems slightly warm to the touch. The card's illustration shows a skull on one face and a radiant sun with a calm human face on the other.

### Properties

Once per long rest, when Elvva hits a creature with an attack, she can flip the coin before damage is dealt:

- **Heads** - the target takes double damage
- **Tails** - Elvva takes the damage instead (at normal value)

### Notes

- Item card supplied by Ant (Nov 2026); the "(at normal value)" detail for tails is from play, not the card
- The table talked her out of flipping it on her 53-damage Fireball in S42
