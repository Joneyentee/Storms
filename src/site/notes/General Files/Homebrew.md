## Ser Alric's Special Ale & Feast (Session 43)

**Source:** [[NPCs/Ser Alric Vayne\|Ser Alric Vayne]], at the [[Locations/Thalas'Miriel\|Thalas'Miriel]] tavern | **Type:** Random magical consumables (DM effect table, like [[General Files/Homebrew#Tooo-Ah's Magical Tea\|Tooo-Ah's tea]])

**Mechanics:** The ale tastes of the drinker's favourite flavour. Each drink, and each plate from the feast that followed, is a d20 roll on the DM's table; drinking also calls for Constitution saves as the night goes on. Rolling the same number again lets the player reroll or go one up or down. Most effects last 24 hours.

**Known effects (drinks):**

|Roll|Effect|
|---|---|
|6|Hair turns bright pink and smells of cheese|
|10|A thunderous burp that shatters nearby glass|
|13|A moustache grows that wiggles when danger is near; repeat drinks add more|
|15|Your next in-character sentence comes true|
|16|Your tankard refills itself when emptied, then floats after you, refilling, for 24 hours|
|17|Dramatic Death Syndrome - act out a mortal wound whenever you move|
|19|Eloquia - eloquent speech, Charisma raised with advantage on Charisma rolls, disadvantage on Wisdom and Intelligence (stacks)|

**Known effects (feast):** duck - two dogs follow you, fascinated; cake - your stomach talks loudly about everything, or everyone must call you "Your Deliciousness"; steak - bonus AC against the next attack made on you; pickles - skin turns green, bonus on the next Acrobatics check; bread - a loaf appears on your head and stays until someone else eats from it; sausages - an urge to share them with everyone.

**End of the night (d6 for the last two standing):** 5 - you wake having adopted an animal (see [[NPCs/Guy 2\|Guy 2]]); other entries included matching tattoos (1), believing you are long-lost siblings (3), promising to help the tavern keeper, and starting a tavern-wide conga line through the town (6).

**Note:** Roll numbers are as heard on the recording; several rolls overlapped at the table, so treat the mapping as approximate.

## Odonia's Warg Ability

**Type:** Homebrew (replaces druid multiclass) **Acquired:** Session 30 (green Minotaur ooze transformation during dream vision)

### Mechanics (Revealed Session 31)

**Tattoo:** Shifting tattoo on chest (wolf/eagle/serpent morphs constantly)

**Warging:**

- Project consciousness into creatures within 30ft
- Body falls unconscious while warging
- No psychic damage when warging ends (creature dies or cancelled)

**Control Mechanic (Session 31):**

- Roll grey die when warging
- **Even result** = Full control over creature
- **Odd result** = Creature's hostile instincts take over, may attack allies

**Spirit-Bonded Creature Discovery (Session 36):**

- Attempting to Warg into a creature that is spiritually bonded to another being (e.g., the orc rider's Frost Wolf) does not grant control
- Instead: caster is pulled into a **green spirit realm** and meets the bonded creature's spiritual owner
- The spiritual owner can attack directly in this realm - [[Party Members/Lady Odonia of Galantri\|Lady Odonia of Galantri]] took **12 psychic damage** from the orc rider's spirit
- Warg attempt fails; caster is ejected from the spirit realm
- Implies Warg ability interacts with spiritual/Bane-adjacent forces, not just animal minds

**First Combat Test (Session 31):**

- Warging into Earthbound Dire Wolf
- Rolled 3 (odd number)
- Lost control, wolf attacked [[Party Members/Ser Tessell Highfrost\|Ser Tessell Highfrost]] for 15 damage instead of intended target

**Origin:**

- Crushed under debris during Khorrak battle in dream vision
- Khorrak's corrupted green ooze seeped under rock to unconscious body
- Body whisped away in green smoke upon transformation
- Transformation triggered by: prolonged exposure + vulnerable state + corrupted entity death nearby

## Bane Watcher Mechanics

**Acquisition:** Survive dragon blood ritual

**The Ritual:**

- Drink dragon blood mixture (from ancient dragon god)
- Constitution save (DC unknown)
- **Failure:** Damage, extended unconsciousness (4-20 hours), possible death
- **Success:** Still unconscious 4-20 hours
- All experience shared prophetic vision

**Party Results (Session 18):**

- [[Party Members/Elik'r Stormswallow\|Elik'r Stormswallow]]: Nat 20, 20hr sleep
- [[Party Members/Elvva Beltavar\|Elvva Beltavar]]: 20, ~6hr sleep

- [[Party Members/Ser Tessell Highfrost\|Ser Tessell Highfrost]]: 13, ~6hr sleep
- [[Party Members/Lady Odonia of Galantri\|Lady Odonia of Galantri]]: Failed, 5 damage, 4hr sleep (woke first)

**Ability:**

- Permanent passive sense of [[NPCs/The Bane\|The Bane]]'s presence
- Always active, cannot be turned off
- **Current:** Overwhelming jumble (like hearing all prayers)
- **Development:** Improves with time/training
- No mechanical bonus yet (story/RP ability)

### Green Glow Manifestation

**Pattern:** Recurring supernatural green glow/ooze associated with [[NPCs/The Bane\|The Bane]]

**Observed Instances:**

**Session 24:**

- Enhanced [[Party Members/Tooo-Ah\|Tooo-Ah]]'s Scorching Ray spell
- Green glow on hands during casting
- Spell dealt extra damage

**Session 25:**

- Triggered [[Party Members/Elvva Beltavar\|Elvva Beltavar]]'s temporary petrification
- Green glow emanated from body
- Unclear if cause or symptom

**Session 26:**

- Skeletal soldier departed in "eerie green light"
- Possible Bane-corrupted entity
- Teleportation method?

**Session 30:**

- **Khorrak disintegrated into green ooze**
- Seeped under rock to transform [[Party Members/Lady Odonia of Galantri\|Lady Odonia of Galantri]]
- Green smoke whisped body away
- Resulted in Warg ability (Session 30)

**Session 34:**

- **Elvva's spell reflection pattern confirmed**
- Wisdom saving throws required during spellcasting with green glow
- First two failed saves: resisted (Constitution saves 19 and 25 while holding Fireball)
- Third failed save (rolled 5): full spell damage reflected back
- Aganazzar's Scorcher (32 damage) reflected, knocked unconscious at moment of victory
- Establishes escalating danger pattern with repeated high-level spellcasting

**Session 42:**

- The [[NPCs/Unknown Axe Fighter\|Unknown Axe Fighter]] burned a green Bane rune into the ground; the party's Bane Sense felt it as a physical pull
- The rune empowered four undead champions and visibly drained them as they used it
- Life force (10 HP each) was torn from three of the party as green energy and fired into a dead tree, animating it; killing the tree sent the energy back into the same three as 10 necrotic damage each
- [[Party Members/Elvva Beltavar\|Elvva Beltavar]]'s Fireball turned green as it was cast; no reflection

**Pattern Analysis:**

- Associated with Bane corruption
- Can enhance abilities (Tooo-Ah)
- Can trigger conditions (Elvva)
- Can transform (Odonia)
- **Can reflect damage (Elvva - 3-strike pattern)**
- Green = consistent supernatural marker
- Progressive escalation with repeated exposure
