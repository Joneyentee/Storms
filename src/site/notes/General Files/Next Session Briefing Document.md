# Session 44 Briefing - Quick Reference

## What Happened Last Session

[[NPCs/Lord Pompaduke the Third\|Lord Pompaduke the Third]] stepped out at the cemetery, told Elik'r to warn Co'narr that "I'm coming for him next", and portalled away. A goddess's voice led [[Party Members/Ser Tessell Highfrost\|Ser Tessell Highfrost]] to the mausoleum, whose lantern door opened to his prayer: below lay the [[Locations/Tomb of Ser Elenai\|Tomb of Ser Elenai]], the First Lantern and founder of the [[Organisations/Order of the Silver Lantern\|Order of the Silver Lantern]], and a prophecy about Tessell. We passed her five trials - and [[Party Members/Lady Odonia of Galantri\|Lady Odonia of Galantri]] confessed out loud that she killed the king - and Ser Elenai gave Tessell her glaive, making him the "First Light". Back in [[Locations/Thalas'Miriel\|Thalas'Miriel]], [[NPCs/Ser Alric Vayne\|Ser Alric Vayne]] pledged the Order to [[NPCs/Calen Thorne\|Calen Thorne]]'s claim, threw a magical ale night ([[NPCs/Guy 2\|Guy 2]] the goat is now real), and we set off for [[Locations/Neverwinter\|Neverwinter]] with 104 Silver Lanterns behind us.

**Our Current Mission:** Get to [[Locations/Neverwinter\|Neverwinter]], beat or undo [[NPCs/High Luminary Aelthir Vaelorian\|High Luminary Aelthir Vaelorian]]'s delegation, and put Calen on the ducal throne - without killing his brothers.

**The First Lantern's Glaive - quick reminder:**

- 1d8 one-handed (shield in the other hand) or 1d12 two-handed with 10ft reach
- Sheds light that magical darkness can only halve; allies within 10ft have advantage on saves against being frightened
- Two-handed: +1 damage, and once per turn on a hit spend a Channel Divinity to Dispel Magic the target
- **Lantern's Sanctuary** (1/day, action, two hands): 30ft sphere for 1 minute - dispels magical darkness, allies +1 AC and advantage vs curses and fear, allies heal 1d4 + Wis at the start of their turn, undead and fiends take 1d6 radiant
- **Warding Glow** (2/day, reaction): resistance to one hit on you or an ally within 30ft; undead or fiend attackers take 1d6 radiant
