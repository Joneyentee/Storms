# Session 41 Briefing - Quick Reference

## What Happened Last Session

We agreed to help [[NPCs/Calen Thorne\|Calen Thorne]] pursue the Ducal claim, and he joined us properly as a travelling party member — but before we could leave, we found [[Locations/Thalas'Miriel\|Thalas'Miriel]] deserted and overrun. The [[Organisations/Order of the Silver Lantern\|Order of the Silver Lantern]] garrison was barricaded shut, now under [[NPCs/Ser Alric Vayne\|Ser Alric Vayne]]'s command after [[NPCs/Darius Kain\|Darius Kain]]'s death, and Vayne told us fast-moving "zombies" had driven his people back toward the cathedral. We found the source near the town inn: [[NPCs/Lord Pompaduke the Third\|Lord Pompaduke the Third]], directing the attack from a rooftop while playing his lute. We fought through wave after wave of undead, silenced and blinded Pompaduke to shut down his magic, and [[Party Members/Elik'r Stormswallow\|Elik'r Stormswallow]] finally attuned to the Stormswallow longsword — discovering it can slam into the ground to unleash a wave of fire. The fight was still raging when Vayne arrived with reinforcements, [[Party Members/Tooo-Ah\|Tooo-Ah]] in tow.

**Our Current Mission:** Finish off Pompaduke and his zombies, secure Thalas'Miriel, then get to [[Locations/Neverwinter\|Neverwinter]] to lock down Duke Alaric Thorne's allegiance before Vaelorian's delegation gets there first.
