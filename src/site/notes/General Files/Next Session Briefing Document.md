# Session 42 Briefing - Quick Reference

## What Happened Last Session

We finished off [[NPCs/Lord Pompaduke the Third\|Lord Pompaduke the Third]] and his zombies at [[Locations/Thalas'Miriel\|Thalas'Miriel]] - or so we thought. [[Party Members/Lady Odonia of Galantri\|Lady Odonia of Galantri]]'s Radiant Bow destroyed what turned out to be a decoy; his true, invisible self had been elsewhere on the battlefield the whole time. He bowed and portalled away with "See you at our next chapter!" [[NPCs/Ser Alric Vayne\|Ser Alric Vayne]] is now openly in command of the garrison. We rested in the town's tavern (now a field hospital), where [[NPCs/Calen Thorne\|Calen Thorne]] confirmed to [[Party Members/Tooo-Ah\|Tooo-Ah]] that he intends to claim the Ducal throne of Neverwinter, and [[Party Members/Elik'r Stormswallow\|Elik'r Stormswallow]] rang up the Steve stone - the voice on the other end, recognisably [[NPCs/Co'narr 'Banewatcher' Hyborian\|Co'narr 'Banewatcher' Hyborian]], warned us not to trust Pompaduke and revealed he was cast out of Co'narr's own order decades ago. We then rode out to a cemetery south-west of town - the actual source of the zombie incursion - and found four figures in a clandestine meeting at an old mausoleum: the [[NPCs/Ember Vein Envoy - Unnamed\|Ember Vein Envoy - unnamed]], Pompaduke himself (again), the [[NPCs/Mysterious Cloaked Man\|Mysterious Cloaked Man]], and the [[NPCs/Unknown Axe Fighter\|Unknown Axe Fighter]]. The Envoy told us "the other dragon you awakened" is on **Dragonclaw Isle**, then left for Neverwinter. Pompaduke bowed and portalled away a second time. The Cloaked Man cast a necromancy spell - four bolts of green Bane light - and vanished. The Axe Fighter stayed behind and challenged us to combat. The session ended on the initiative roll.

**Our Current Mission:** Fight (or talk our way past) the Axe Fighter, work out what to do about Dragonclaw Isle, then get to [[Locations/Neverwinter\|Neverwinter]] to lock down Duke Alaric Thorne's allegiance before Vaelorian's delegation gets there first.
