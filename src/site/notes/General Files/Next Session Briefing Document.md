# Session 43 Briefing - Quick Reference

## What Happened Last Session

Session 42 was one long fight at the cemetery mausoleum. The [[NPCs/Unknown Axe Fighter\|Unknown Axe Fighter]] burned a rune of [[NPCs/The Bane\|The Bane]] into the ground and raised four undead champions - the Veiled Oracle, the Hollow Blade, the Oathless and the Last Huntress. A 4th-level Fireball left most of us bloodied in the first round, [[Party Members/Elvva Beltavar\|Elvva Beltavar]] was shot down to 1 HP, and both [[Party Members/Ser Tessell Highfrost\|Ser Tessell Highfrost]] and [[Party Members/Elik'r Stormswallow\|Elik'r Stormswallow]] hit 0 HP at different points. We destroyed all four champions and a Bane-animated tree, and Tessell's Command ("Retreat") made the Axe Fighter portal away. A zombie horde then surrounded us; Tessell's hero-token Turn Undead sent it running. As it fled, a lute started playing: [[NPCs/Lord Pompaduke the Third\|Lord Pompaduke the Third]] never left in Session 41 - he has been invisible beside the mausoleum the whole time.

**Our Current Mission:** Deal with Pompaduke here and now, finish with the cemetery, work out what to do about Dragonclaw Isle, then get to [[Locations/Neverwinter\|Neverwinter]] before Vaelorian's delegation turns the Duke.
