## White Die

**MAJOR UPDATE (Session 36):** The white die is NOT Elvva-specific. Ant says explicitly, mid-session, when Odonia attempts to Warg into a Frost Wolf already bonded to someone else:

"So you've got a white dice that I gave you all. I need to roll it." (rolled 4) - immediately followed by Odonia taking 12 psychic damage as the Warg attempt fails.

This means every party member has been given one of these white dice, and it ties into more than one character-specific mechanic (Odonia's Warg ability AND Elvva's Bane corruption/reflection).

**First identified:** Session 34 (Moth Queen Cavern), during Elvva's Scorching Ray.

"I need you to roll your white dice with the roman numerals on five. On the hit, a target takes two D6 plus..."

(Transcription software rendered "roman numerals" as "aroma numerals" - likely why it was never flagged during summary-writing.)

**Confirmed again, Session 36:** Twice in one session -
1. Odonia's failed Warg attempt (see above) - die roll directly preceded a damage/failure consequence.
2. Elvva's Dragon's Breath (level 2) - "Can you roll your white dice please?" (rolled 3) - cast turned green, hit Odonia and an orc.

**Confirmed again, Session 40:** During Elvva's Chromatic Orb cast against Lord Pompaduke - rolled once (result: 4), alongside a separate green die instruction.

**Working theory (revised):** The white die appears to be a general-purpose hidden mechanic given to ALL party members, used by Ant at his discretion across different character-specific triggers - not exclusively tied to Elvva's Bane corruption. It may function as a roll Ant uses whenever a character's individual "thing the Bane/world has hooked into them" might matter - Odonia's Warg control, Elvva's corruption, potentially others not yet observed (Tessell's palm manifestation? Elik'r's guilt curse?).

**Checked and ruled out:** Session 34's actual reflection moment (Agonising Scorcher, 32 damage back to Elvva) - no die colour mentioned at that exact point; played as a standard Wisdom save.

## Green Die

**First identified:** Session 40, two separate instances, both against Lord Pompaduke:

1. Chromatic Orb, level 2: "Roll your white numbered dice please" (4) then "make sure to roll your green dice twice for me" (1, 1)
2. Fireball, level 3: "roll that green token a further three times" (7, 6, 3) - added 16 bonus damage on top of base 26, for the 42-damage hit referenced in Memorable Moments ("Countered Right Back")

**Checked, not found:** Session 36 - no green die mention anywhere in this transcript, despite multiple Elvva spell casts (Dragon's Breath, Chromatic Orb, Sorcerer's Burst). Still appears to be Session 40-only so far - may be a newer mechanic than the white die, or simply not triggered in S36's specific spells.

**Working theory:** Rolled a number of times equal to spell level; adds bonus damage. Elik'r's own dialogue in S40 ("3 green dice... at level 3") confirms this has been a standing mechanic for some time, not a one-off - so it likely predates S40, just hasn't surfaced yet in sessions checked so far.

**Paul's theory (unconfirmed):** Rolling the "wrong" number/parity, or the same number repeatedly, could cause the bonus damage to backfire onto Elvva (and allies, if AoE). Not yet evidenced in transcripts checked so far.
