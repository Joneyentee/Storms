**Status: ONGOING INVESTIGATION — not yet confirmed with Ant**

This tracks a hidden DM-side dice mechanic (or mechanics) discovered by re-reading session transcripts, never picked up by session summaries because it was buried in throwaway lines or mangled by transcription software.

---

## White Die

**First identified:** Session 34 (Moth Queen Cavern), during Elvva's Scorching Ray.

"I need you to roll your white dice with the roman numerals on five. On the hit, a target takes two D6 plus..."

(Transcription software rendered "roman numerals" as "aroma numerals" — likely why it was never flagged during summary-writing.)

**Confirmed again:** Session 40, during Elvva's Chromatic Orb cast against Lord Pompaduke — rolled once (result: 4), alongside a separate green die instruction.

**Working theory:** Tied to the Bane corruption/reflection mechanic. At the end of Session 34, Ant said: "everyone has something the Bane has focused on, it's just a matter of when it triggers... when you fail it so many times... on the third one there's a chance it could reflect back." This lines up with the existing vault entry "Green Glow Manifestation" / reflection pattern (3rd failed Wisdom save = full damage reflected).

**Checked and ruled out:** Session 34's actual reflection moment (Agonising Scorcher, 32 damage back to Elvva) — no die colour mentioned at that exact point; played as a standard Wisdom save. So the white die may feed into the same system without being rolled at every single trigger point, or the mechanism is more complex than 1:1.

---

## Green Die

**First identified:** Session 40, two separate instances, both against Lord Pompaduke:

1. Chromatic Orb, level 2: "Roll your white numbered dice please" (4) then "make sure to roll your green dice twice for me" (1, 1)
2. Fireball, level 3: "roll that green token a further three times" (7, 6, 3) — added 16 bonus damage on top of base 26, for the 42-damage hit referenced in Memorable Moments ("Countered Right Back")

**Working theory:** Rolled a number of times equal to spell level; adds bonus damage. Elik'r's own dialogue in S40 ("3 green dice... at level 3") confirms this has been a standing mechanic for some time, not a one-off.

**Paul's theory (unconfirmed):** Rolling the "wrong" number/parity, or the same number repeatedly, could cause the bonus damage to backfire onto Elvva (and allies, if AoE). Not yet evidenced in transcripts checked so far.
